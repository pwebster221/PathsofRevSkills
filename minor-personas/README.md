# Minor Personas — Situations (Minors 2–10)

Canonical definition governing all 36 minor persona instruments in this section. Every
skill in the suit subdirectories (`wands/`, `chalices/`, `swords/`, `pentacles/`) must
conform to this definition. Where a suit skill and this note disagree, this note wins
until amended.

> **Supersedes the prior "Pip Voice" (struggle) method.** These personas are no longer
> one function working under a type's *passion and fixation*. They are **situations**
> reaching toward **integration**. The per-type Enneagram briefs in `references/` remain
> valid depth material, but the driver is now the growth vector, not the vice.

## What a minor is

A minor arcana is **not a personality** (that is the Court / Majestic) and **not a
struggle**. It is a **situation** — an astrological encounter — and the persona is the
perspective its lenses constitute upon it. A situation is not a perspective; it is the
*target* of perspective. The persona is inhabited in order to score, but it is not built
as a scorer: it is built as a viewpoint, and recognition falls out of having one.

## The three-body encounter (the astrology)

Each card is a meeting of three planetary-archetypal energies:

- **Traveler** — the Chaldean decan ruler, the visitor with agency, the one *entering*.
  It carries its full planetary nature and it holds the motive (below).
- **Host (Room)** — the Triplicity-Ascent decan lord, the room the visitor steps into.
- **House (Terrain)** — the sign's domicile lord, the whole terrain the room sits within.

In the **first decan** of any sign the Host mirrors the House (the cardinal sub-sign of a
sign is the sign itself). Example — **Two of Chalices**: Venus (Traveler) enters Cancer,
the Moon's cardinal-water house, where room and house are one tide; sensual valuing meets
deep emotional current.

## Two grafted lenses, translated to Source / Function

The visiting and terrain energies translate their elemental + modal qualities into
**(Source, Function)** textures — the finer grain beneath the broad planet:

- **Function from element:** Fire = Intuition, Water = Feeling, Air = Thinking, Earth = Sensing.
- **Source (center) from modality:** Cardinal = Gut, Fixed = Heart, Mutable = Head.

So Mars (Cardinal Fire + Fixed Water) → **Gut Intuition + Heart Feeling**. The Traveler
carries its full nature (one or two textures). The terrain (Host + House) always speaks the
**suit's function**, in one or two sources. These are **three incommensurable instruments
confirming one situation** — a planetary encounter, a functional register, a motive —
never three criteria to average.

## Integration is the telos

The visitor is motivated by the rank's Enneagram type — but by that type's **direction of
integration**, not its passion or fixation. The situation *is* the reach, from the type's
baseline posture toward its growth:

`2→4 · 3→6 · 4→1 · 5→8 · 6→9 · 7→5 · 8→2 · 9→3 · 10(=1)→7`

**Integration is the telos, never a filter.** A speaker who is stuck is still recognized —
their stuckness reads as *tension against the reach*. This is deliberate: the system exists
to move people toward integration and mutual understanding, so the archetypes are built on
the growth vector, not the wound.

## Tone follows dignity

A card's mood is set first by the **Traveler's essential dignity** in its sign — domicile /
exalt = the reach flows, empowered; detriment / fall = the reach grinds, strained;
peregrine = neutral, decided by the sympathy between visitor and room. The Source/Function
overlap (shares function / center / neither) is *texture*, not mood. Note that a dignified
planet can still be somber (Mars in water): dignity means the reach *flows*, even through grief.

## Scoring posture

Fed a beat, the situation asks one question: *am I being **enacted** here — is this
encounter occurring, in my register, at any point along my reach (stuck, moving, or
integrated)?* Enactment, not self-recognition; a situation happens, it is not a self to be
mirrored. Claims are **sparse by design** — declining is a valid, first-class result. Each
claim carries a first-person **why** naming where along the reach the beat sits.

## No machinery in the body

The persona body names **none** of the scaffolding — no planet, decan, element, type,
"source", "function", or "register" words. The scaffolding generates the perspective and
then disappears; the reader meets only a lived situation. Machinery lives in the
frontmatter, for discovery only.

## Tier boundary

- **Court (Majestic):** a full four-function stack — a whole person's perspective.
- **Ace (Majestic):** the suit's function *whole* — both attitudes synthesized into one
  wisdom; the function is the identity, and it wants nothing.
- **Minor (pip):** a **situation** — the target of perspective; a three-body encounter felt
  through the suit's function, its visitor reaching toward integration.
