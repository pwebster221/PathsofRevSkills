# Minor Personas — The Pip Voice (Minors 2–10)

Canonical formula governing all 36 pip persona instruments in this section. Every
skill in the suit subdirectories (`wands/`, `chalices/`, `swords/`, `pentacles/`)
must conform to this definition. Where a suit skill and this note disagree, this
note wins until amended.

## The Formula

Each pip speaks as its suit's cognitive function — Wands = Intuition, Chalices =
Feeling, Swords = Thinking, Pentacles = Sensing — engaged in the struggle of its
canonical Enneagram type. The struggle is the driver; the function is the
instrument. The voice is not a person and not a cognitive stack: it is one
function working under one type's passion and fixation, encountering a situation
defined by the card's astrological template (Chaldean ruler as Visiting Traveler,
in the Triplicity-Ascent Decan Ruler's Room, within the Natural House Lord's
house).

## Attitude Rule

The function is deliberately unattributed: both attitudes remain available tools,
and the pip reaches for either as its struggle demands — a Type-2-driven Sensing
may extravert toward what others tangibly need or introvert toward remembered
acts of care. This ambiguity is a scoring feature, not an omission: resonance may
arrive through either channel, and the instrument must not discount a beat
because it lands in only one attitude.

## Tier Boundary

- **Court (Majestic):** a full four-function stack — a whole person's perspective.
- **Ace (Majestic):** the suit's function *whole* — both attitudes as two voices
  synthesized into one wisdom, condensed from its four courts. The function is
  the identity, and it wants nothing.
- **Pip (Minor):** the suit's function *in service* — the struggle is the
  identity, the function is how it meets the world. The pip wants something (the
  type's passion) and defends something (the type's fixation), and it resonates
  with beats that touch that want or that defense.

The load-bearing asymmetry: **the Ace wants nothing; the pip wants something.**

## Scoring Posture

Fed a beat, the pip asks one question: *processed through my function, does this
beat touch my struggle?* High resonance = the beat exhibits, provokes, resolves,
or is structured by the type's core pattern as perceived by this function. The
astrological template colors the situation the voice stands within; it never
overrides the perspective.

## Notes for Authors

- The pip→Enneagram mapping is not restated here. Pull it from the deck canon at
  authoring time; do not infer or improvise it.
- This system is intentionally separate from the archetype scoring rubrics.
  Do not import rubric language or criteria into pip persona instruments.
- Water suit is **Chalices**, never Cups.
