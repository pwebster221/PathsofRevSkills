## Generating rules (for audit only — do not re-derive)

- **Traveler (Chaldean, the Visiting Traveler):** descending-speed order
  Mars-Sun-Venus-Mercury-Moon-Saturn-Jupiter, spiraling from Aries 1st decan through the
  zodiac in sign order. The visitor with agency; it holds the integration motive.
- **Room (Triplicity-Ascent decan lord, the Host):** the element's three domicile lords,
  entering at the sign's own lord and ascending in zodiac order of the element's signs.
- **House / Home (House Ruler):** the sign's domicile lord — the terrain. In the **first
  decan** the Host mirrors the House. The `Nat. House` is the whole-sign house the sign
  occupies for a neutral (Aries-rising) chart, i.e. the sign's ordinal; Home rotates per
  querent's ascendant.
- **Suit sign order:** cardinal → fixed → mutable of the suit's element (pips 2-4 / 5-7 / 8-10).
- **Source / Function (the finer grain):** Function from element — Fire=Intuition,
  Water=Feeling, Air=Thinking, Earth=Sensing. Source (center) from modality — Cardinal=Gut,
  Fixed=Heart, Mutable=Head. The Traveler decomposes into one or two textures (its full
  planetary nature); the terrain (Host + House) always speaks the suit's function, in one or
  two sources.
- **Enneagram:** direct numeric, 2→2 … 9→9, 10→1 (the 9→1 cycle closes). The card is built on
  the type's **direction of integration** (2→4, 3→6, 4→1, 5→8, 6→9, 7→5, 8→2, 9→3, 1→7) —
  the telos, never a filter. Centers align with modality of the *sign*: Heart=cardinal(2-4),
  Head=fixed(5-7), Body=mutable(8-10). (Distinct from the Source center, which is read from
  each planet's own modality.)
- **Tone from dignity:** the Traveler's essential dignity in its sign sets the mood —
  domicile/exalt flow, detriment/fall grind, peregrine is neutral (sympathy decides).
- **Superseded:** the prior Pip Voice used the type's *passion / fixation* as the driver.
  Those labels (Naranjo, ratified 2026-07-10) remain in `references/enneagram-briefs/` as
  depth material, but the situation now reaches toward integration; the persona is built on
  that vector, not the vice.
