**Provenance & verification (2026-07-04):** Traveler and Room pulled from the Esoteric
Repository (`COUNTER_RULED_BY` / `RULED_BY_DECAN` edges on all 36 `:MinorArcana` nodes),

and independently re-derived from the generating rules below. All three sources agree on
all 36 cards. Authoring agents MUST use this table verbatim — never re-derive or infer.

## Generating rules (for audit only — do not re-derive)

- **Traveler (Chaldean, the Visiting Traveler):** descending-speed order
  Mars-Sun-Venus-Mercury-Moon-Saturn-Jupiter, spiraling from Aries 1st decan through
  the zodiac in sign order.
- **Room (Triplicity-Ascent Ruler, canonical decan system):** the element's three
  domicile lords, entering at the sign's own lord, ascending in zodiac order of the
  element's signs.
- **Home (House Ruler):** personal layer — lord of the whole-sign house the card's sign
  occupies for the querent's ascendant. Neutral chart (Aries rising) collapses it onto
  the sign's domicile lord (the "Natural Lord" column). Skills describe the neutral
  default and note that Home rotates per querent.
- **Suit sign order:** cardinal → fixed → mutable of the suit's element
  (pips 2-4 / 5-7 / 8-10).
- **Enneagram:** direct numeric, 2→2 … 9→9, 10→1 (the 9→1 cycle closes). Centers align
  with modality: Heart=cardinal(2-4), Head=fixed(5-7), Body=mutable(8-10).
- **Passion/Fixation labels** below are placeholder seeds (Naranjo-standard terms +
  the graph's `psych_enneagram_shadow` arrays). The research phase replaces them with
  sourced Naranjo (structure) and Palmer (voice) material; they are NOT authoritative.

## Graph notes

- `:MinorArcana` nodes carry `psych_enneagram_center` and `psych_enneagram_shadow` but
  **no explicit enneagram-type property** — the type lives in the number mapping above.
- `planetary_court_ruler` / `planetary_court_seat` on pip nodes belong to the planetary
  court / sephirothic system — unrelated to the MBTI courts. Do not conflate.
- `psych_mbti_type` is empty on all pips — correct; pips are not MBTI speakers.
