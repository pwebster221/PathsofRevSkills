# Generative Principle (UNC exploration)

This skill runs the **research and brainstorming** side of the Unified Numeric Cosmology:
the claim that the numbers **1 through 21** form a single generative pattern, each number a
distinct operation in the deck's (and cosmos's) self-generation. The endpoint is 21 because
the Major Arcana count is 21 (the Fool is 0, standing outside the count). The work is hosted
in the Linear project **Generative Principle** — one issue per number, deepened as exploration
accrues, plus cross-cutting "discovery" issues for patterns that span numbers.

This is **derivation, not lookup**. The static correspondence tables that `tarot-interpretation`
reads are *downstream* of this work. Here we are still building the structure: positing,
testing, deepening, and occasionally overturning.

## When to use

- "Explore / deepen number N" — work a single rung in its own depth.
- "What's the generative move into N?" / "from N to M?" — work a *movement* (an edge).
- "Brainstorm the generative principle" / open-ended UNC ideation.
- "Is this pattern consistent with the UNC?" — test a new observation against the grammar.
- "Where would this discovery live?" — route a finding to the right Linear issue.
- Synthesizing across numbers (pillars, septenaries, the two grammars, prime theory).

**Not** for interpreting drawn cards (→ `tarot-interpretation`), nor for non-numeric esoteric
synthesis (→ `esoteric-exploration`).

## The two operational grammars

Two grammars generate the sequence; together they may be complete (ESO-22 open hypothesis).
Full treatment in `references/operational-grammars.md`.

1. **Multiplicative — Operator × Substance → Result** (ESO-14). Handles **composites**.
   Smaller factor = **Operator** (the kind of traversal / initiating will), larger factor =
   **Substance** (the structure being traversed / materia held steady), product = **Result**
   (the emergent Third, not reducible to the other two). Maps to **Cardinal · Fixed · Mutable**.
   A square (4, 9, 16) is **self-action** — a number operating on itself.
   - *Composite decomposition rule:* the prefactor names a **kind of traversal**; the number
     multiplied is the **structure traversed**. For odd composites the prefactor is 3 (trinity)
     acting on a doubled-prime ground (the reach-back mechanism).

2. **Sequential — "moving through"** (ESO-22). Handles **primes** (which the multiplicative
   grammar cannot recover). An active term **moves along/through** prior integers and lifts out
   an emergent term: provisional form **A active + B passive → A + B + 1** (the +1 is the
   abstracted trinity-Third). E.g. 7 on 7 → 15; 10 on 10 → 21; 1 along 9 → 11 (Da'ath);
   1 along 12 → 13 (Ophiuchus). The "+1" anomaly for 13 is an open question.

**The Result is abstraction, not addition** (ESO-14). Nothing is added — there was only One.
The Result is the *relation itself* lifted out and granted its own standing, so it can become
the One of the next cycle. The silent failure mode is **collapse into Two**: an operation runs
but no Third abstracts out, so nothing carries forward.

## The structural frame

Read `references/unc-structure.md` for the master table. The load-bearing structure:

- **Generate / Produce rhythm.** Odd→Even = **Generate** (abstraction posits substance);
  Even→Odd = **Produce** (substance abstracts upward, lifting a Third). Every prime past 2
  arrives on a **Produce** step; **2 is the unique Generate-prime**.
- **Ascending Formula.** Odd = **abstraction**, Even = **productive**. The walk up alternates
  the two moves; "three things produce a fourth" *is* the formula in miniature.
- **Three septenaries** (ESO-27): Operator 1–7 = **Cardinal**, Substance 8–14 = **Fixed**,
  Result 15–21 = **Mutable** — themselves one trinity at macro scale; 21 = the result of the result.
- **Four pillars by base prime** (ESO-33): Mind (×2, the evens), Soul (×3), Man (×5),
  Divine (×7). Dual members (6/12/18 = Mind∧Soul, 10/20 = Mind∧Man, 15 = Soul∧Man,
  14 = Mind∧Divine, 21 = Soul∧Divine) read as **reinforcement**.
- **Prime taxonomy** (ESO-28): pillar-base primes **2,3,5,7** (each *founds* a pillar) vs
  platform/completion primes **11,13,17,19** (completions standing *above* the composites).
  1 is the precondition prime (Source); whether 1 also heads a "Divine" pillar is unresolved.

## Method & standing rule

This project moves at a deliberate pace. Honor it.

1. **One rung at a time, in its own depth** (the Emerald Tablet 2→3 reframing, ESO-53).
   Don't race ahead to number M while N is unworked. Issues exist as **scaffolding, not
   commitments** — proposing structure for an un-deepened number is fine *as conjecture*,
   but flag it as such.
2. **Derive, don't recite.** The point is to *work the grammar* and see what it produces,
   not to repeat known correspondences. Treat numerology, gematria, and tradition as
   **testimony** (evidence to weigh), never as conclusion.
3. **Hold multiple frames at once.** A number is simultaneously: a prime/composite, a
   position in the Generate/Produce walk, a septenary member, a pillar member (often several),
   a node in both grammars, and a Major Arcana correspondent. Read it through all of them; let
   the crossings reinforce or tension each other.
4. **Distinguish established depth from conjecture, always.** Mark what is load-bearing
   (2,3,4,5,6,7,9,12 + the cross-cutting structures), what is a working hypothesis, and what
   is fresh speculation in this turn.
5. **Test against both grammars and the rhythm.** A genuine UNC finding should locate itself
   on the Generate/Produce walk, name its grammar (multiplicative or sequential), and respect
   the pillar/septenary structure — or explicitly say where it breaks them (breaks are valuable).
6. **Movements are first-class.** The edges *to* and *from* a number (e.g. 2→3, 3→4, 7→14,
   3×3=9, 1-along-12→13) carry as much structure as the numbers. When deepening N, work its
   movements, not just N in isolation.

## Working a number (the loop)

1. **Orient** — `references/number-index.md` for the slot, then `get_issue` for the live issue's
   full prose. Note current depth and existing threads/movements/open-questions.
2. **Locate** — prime or composite? Generate or Produce arrival? Which septenary, which
   pillar(s)? Square (self-action)? Platform vs pillar-base prime?
3. **Run the grammar** — for a composite, factor into Operator×Substance→Result and read the
   traversal; for a prime, find the sequential "A along B → prime" move(s). Map to
   Cardinal/Fixed/Mutable.
4. **Work the movements** — derive the rungs into and out of the number; check both directions.
5. **Cross-check frames** — does the reading cohere across pillar, septenary, Major correspondent,
   Enneagram/zodiac shape, and the other grammar? Note reinforcement and tension.
6. **Test the open questions** — pick up the issue's listed Open questions; advance or reframe them.
7. **Mark certainty** and record (below).

## Brainstorming a new pattern

When a candidate pattern appears (often from another exercise — e.g. a rubric pass):

- State it plainly, then **stress-test** it against the grammars, the rhythm, and the pillar/
  septenary structure. Does it predict? (The project's open test: predict a Major's meaning
  from UNC grammar *before* consulting tradition.)
- Check whether it's already an issue (search the project) — extend rather than duplicate.
- Decide scope: does it belong to **one number** (→ that ESO issue) or **span numbers**
  (→ a new cross-cutting discovery issue, in the style of the existing cross-cutting issues)?
- Keep the **open hypothesis** in view: each number 1–21 may be the foundational principle of
  the corresponding Major, with Pips as the function-axis traversal and Courts as the 12 = 3×4
  typological matrix — a unified numeric backbone for the whole 78. Test, don't assume.

## Recording findings

Persist only when the user wants it kept; confirm the target first.

- **Deepening an existing number** → update its ESO issue. Read the current body
  (`get_issue`), then write the *merged* body back with `save_issue` (preserve existing
  sections; append/extend rather than overwrite). Use real newlines in markdown, not `\n`.
  For lighter notes, `linear_create_comment` on the issue.
- **A cross-number discovery** → create a new issue in the project (`linear_create_issue`,
  `project: "Rungs"`, team `ESO`, label **Esoteric Research**), titled like the
  existing discovery issues, with a *Discovery context* (where/when it surfaced), *The
  structure*, *Per-number evidence*, *Relationship to existing grammar*, and *Open* sections.
- **Cross-reference** related issues by their ESO id in the body, the way the project already does.
- Don't change a number's stated **Status** ("Loosely defined" → "Established depth") unless the
  work genuinely reaches depth and the user confirms the promotion.

```
# Generative Principle — <number / movement / pattern>

## Slot
<prime|composite · Generate/Produce arrival · septenary/modality · pillar(s) · Major correspondent>

## Grammar
<multiplicative Op×Sub→Result, or sequential A-along-B→result; mapped to Cardinal/Fixed/Mutable>

## Movements
<the rungs into and out of the number, both directions>

## Reading
<the derivation — what this number/move/pattern DOES in the self-generation>

## Cross-frame check
<reinforcements and tensions across pillar / septenary / Major / other grammar>

## Certainty
<established depth | working hypothesis | fresh conjecture — per claim>

## Linear
<issue updated/created, or "not recorded">
```

Lead with the structural slot, run the grammar explicitly, and let the *derivation* carry the
read — mark every claim's certainty, and respect the one-rung-at-a-time pacing.
