# UNC Structure — master frame

The Unified Numeric Cosmology maps the generative pattern across **1 → 21**. The 21 endpoint is
set by the Major Arcana count (excluding the Fool, who stands outside as 0). The structure proper
is **21 = 3 × 7** — a trinity of septenaries.

## Classification

**9 emergent primes** (Enneagram-shaped) — irreducible generative acts:
`1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19`.

- **1** — Unity / Source, the precondition prime.
- **Pillar-base primes** `2, 3, 5, 7` — each *founds* a pillar (below).
- **Platform / completion primes** `11, 13, 17, 19` — completions standing *above* the composites
  (PAT-478).

**12 generative composites** (Zodiac-shaped) — trinity-operations:
`4, 6, 8, 9, 10, 12, 14, 15, 16, 18, 20, 21`.

## Reading a composite

Smaller factor = **Operator**, larger = **Substance**, product = **Result**
(Operator · Substance → Result), mapped to **Cardinal · Fixed · Mutable**. A square (4, 9, 16) is
**self-action** — the number operating on itself. Full grammar in `operational-grammars.md`.

## Generate / Produce rhythm

Parity alternation: **Odd → Even = Generate**, **Even → Odd = Produce**. The walk up the sequence
alternates the two moves; primes arrive only on a Produce step (2 is the lone Generate-prime).

## Three septenaries → modalities (PAT-470)

The project's milestones are modality progressions, not just sequential groupings:

- **First septenary (1–7) = Operator / Cardinal**
- **Second septenary (8–14) = Substance / Fixed**
- **Third septenary (15–21) = Result / Mutable**

The three septenaries are themselves one Operator·Substance·Result trinity at the macro scale;
The World (21) is **the result of the result**.
