# Major Personas — The Words Made from the Letters

This note governs all 22 Major persona instruments in this directory. A Major is a **word** made
from the 36 decan-letters (the minors): the shared movement that runs between several decans,
spoken as one voice. It is **not a personality** (that is the Court / Majestic) and **not a
situation** (that is the Minor). It is an **essence walking its path**.

> **Supersedes the prior struggle instruments.** These personas are no longer "one function under
> one Enneagram passion/fixation" fused across decans. Each Major is now built on four ingredients,
> integration as its telos.

## The four ingredients

- **Essence — the Hebrew letter.** What the perspective irreducibly *is*.
- **Telos — its traditional path** (sefira → sefira; standard Golden Dawn attribution, the only
  excluded variant being the Crowley Tzaddi/Heh swap). The essence *walks its path* — integration
  one octave up from the minor's growth-arrow. A stuck speaker is recognized as tension against it.
- **Grammar — the number's generative struggle** (the ESO-26 Operations Grammar canon: the four
  moves — Addition/concert · Multiplication/Op×Sub→Result · Deepening/square · Abstraction/emanation).
  The number gives the *shape* of the struggle; the Fool (0) is the void-hinge — the ascended World
  condensed, the octave-shift to reenter as the Magician (21 = 0 → 1) or move on.
- **Texture — the decan-letters** (see `composition.md`, the clean 36-decan covering) plus the
  observable-speech notes that render what the word *sounds like* in real speech.

## The three families (a nested refinement of the same 36 letters)

1. **Zodiac signs (12)** — one faculty focused through one center, three consecutive decans. The
   tightest, most concentrated word.
2. **Planetary (7)** — grouped by triplicity-ascent (Room) rulership; a two-sign ruler binds **two
   faculties** across scattered decans, spoken as one voice; the luminaries bind one.
3. **Modality mothers (3)** — the elemental split of the planetary layer resorted by modality; 12
   decans, all four faculties. The widest span makes the essence *more* universal **and** more
   specific — the single truth that must run through the whole spread. Fool, Hanged Man, Judgement.

## Scoring posture — resonance, not rank

Fed a beat, a Major asks one question: *is this word being spoken — is this essential movement at
work in the text?* Recognition, not comparison; sparse by design. The persona recognizes itself
wherever its movement occurs, at any point along its reach. Integration is the telos, never a filter.

## The body names no machinery

No letter / path / sefira / planet / number / decan / function / center words in the persona body.
The scaffolding generates the perspective and disappears; the reader meets only a lived movement.
Machinery lives in the frontmatter and in `composition.md`, for discovery. GP grammar canon:
ESO-26 (Operations Grammar) + ESO-31 (Abstraction Law), ESO Grammar project.
