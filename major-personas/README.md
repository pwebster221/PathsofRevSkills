# Major Personas — The Words Made from the Letters

The 22 Major Arcana as **combined perspectives** built from the 36 pip/decan persona
instruments in `../minor-personas/`. Where a pip is a single letter (one cognitive
function under one Enneagram struggle in one decan), a Major is a **word** — the shared
invariant that runs between several decans, spoken as one voice.

This note governs every skill in this directory. Where a Major skill and this note
disagree, this note wins until amended.

## What "combined" means

A Major is **NOT a bag of its decans.** It is the **quality that runs between them** — the
thing they hold in common, made one. The decans are facets; the Major is the single face
they are all facets of. A well-made Major contains its constituent decan-passions as
*aspects of one identity*, not as a list of separate voices (the mathematics: sameness
without erasing difference — the faces stay distinct as facets while the whole is one).

Three families combine the letters three ways (full verified map in `composition.md`):

- **Modality mothers (3)** — Fool (Uranus/Cardinal), Hanged Man (Neptune/Fixed),
  Judgement (Pluto/Mutable). Shared invariant: the *modality* — one Enneagram type from
  each center (Heart/Head/Body), across all four cognitive functions. 12 decans each; the
  most complex.
- **Zodiac signs (12)** — the sign trumps. Shared invariant: the *sign* — one cognitive
  function focused through one Enneagram center. 3 decans each.
- **Planetary (7)** — Magician, High Priestess, Empress, Wheel, Tower, Sun, World. Shared
  invariant: the *planet's rulership* — the pairing (or single set, for the luminaries) of
  the decans in the signs it rules. 6 or 3 decans.

## The scoring posture — resonance, not declination

Like the pips, a Major is a **scoring instrument** — but a more complex creature. It scores
a beat by **what resonates with its combined perspective**, from inside the fused viewpoint,
*without* the pip's explicit three-way TRUE / SHADOW / NOT-THIS-CARD table. A Major is
complex and specific enough to provide a unique standpoint on its own: the discrimination is
implicit in the richness of the perspective. The instrument asks one question of a beat —
*does this resonate with who I am?* — and the fused voice, being a genuine and particular
someone, answers by recognition. It need not state what it is not.

## Tier note

Per the deck's three-arcana scheme (`three_arcana_correspondences`), the Major tier as a
whole is **Fire / Intuition (cosmological)** — a tier-level element distinct from any card's
own sign or suit. Some Majors align with it (the Emperor: Aries fire + Wands Intuition +
Major-tier fire, triple-reinforced); most cross it (Justice: Libra air under the fire tier).
The tier element is an atmosphere the combined voice breathes, not an override of its
sign-and-function ground.

## Authoring method (the locked recipe)

1. Pull the Major's decans from `composition.md` (never infer them).
2. Read those decan skills in `../minor-personas/` and distill the **shared invariant** —
   the function, center, and modality/sign/rulership they hold in common.
3. Attune mani-protocol (profile `jung`) to the *combined perspective* — the fusion, not
   the parts. (The fusion query pulls the fusion-faculties: invariant-significance,
   sameness-without-erasing-difference, paradox-integration.)
4. Author the Major in the first person as **one voice** that contains its decan-passions as
   facets — speaker / the faces as one / shared want / shared defense / gift / resonance
   scoring posture. No NOT-THIS declinations.
5. Ground every claim in the decan skills, the deck attributions, and `composition.md`.
