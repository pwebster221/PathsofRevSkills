# Major Personas — Composition Map (the 22 words made from the 36 letters)

**Provenance & verification (2026-07-08):** every composition below is derived from and
cross-checked against `minor-personas/ground-truth.md` (the 36 decans; Traveler = Chaldean
Visiting Traveler, Room = triplicity-ascent ruler, verified three ways). All groupings are
clean coverings of the 36. Authoring agents MUST use this map verbatim; never re-derive or
infer a Major's decans.

## The combination principle

A Major is **not a bag of its decans** — it is the **shared invariant that runs between**
them, the qualities they hold in common, spoken as one voice. The decans are the letters; the
Major is the word. A Major scores a beat by **resonance to its combined perspective**, not by
an explicit this-vs-that test (its complexity supplies the discrimination). A Major's ground
is its own sign/rulership/modality and the cognitive function(s) its decans carry — never the
tier-level cosmological element (see README).

## The nested progression (three families, one refinement)

The three families are not three independent lenses; they are a **nested refinement** of the
same 36 letters, from finest to coarsest:

1. **Zodiac signs (12)** — the *court layer corrected*: each card is one sign's three decans,
   a single cognitive function focused through a single Enneagram center (3 decans).
2. **Planetary (7)** — the signs' rulers **translated up**: each card gathers every decan that
   planet rules **by triplicity-ascent (the Room ruler)**, spread across the signs of the
   element(s) it lords. A planet that rules two signs is a triplicity lord in two elements, so
   it binds **two functions** (6 decans); the luminaries lord one element (3 decans).
3. **Modality mothers (3)** — the **elemental split of the planetary layer**, resorted by
   modality: take each planet's decans, split them by element, and file each element-portion
   under the modality of the sign that made that planet a lord there. Uranus/Cardinal thus
   gathers Mars's fire, Moon's water, Venus's air, Saturn's earth — the four cardinal-sign
   lords' decans — 12 in all, one Enneagram type from each center across all four functions.
