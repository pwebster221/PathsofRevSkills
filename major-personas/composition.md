# Major Personas — Composition Map (the 22 words made from the 36 letters)

**Provenance & verification (2026-07-08):** every composition below is derived from and
cross-checked against `minor-personas/ground-truth.md` (the 36 decans, Traveler/Room/sign
verified three ways). All three groupings are clean coverings of the 36: the modality
mothers partition by pip-triad, the zodiac cards partition by sign, the planetary cards
partition by the domicile ruler of each decan's own sign. Authoring agents MUST use this
map verbatim; never re-derive or infer a Major's decans.

## The combination principle

A Major is **not a bag of its decans** — it is the **shared invariant that runs between
them**, the qualities they hold in common, spoken as one voice. The decans are the letters;
the Major is the word. A Major scores a beat by **resonance to its combined perspective**,
not by an explicit this-vs-that test (its complexity supplies the discrimination). Three
families, three ways of combining:

- **Modality mothers (3):** the shared invariant is the *modality* — one Enneagram type from
  each center, across all four cognitive functions. The most complex: 12 decans each.
- **Zodiac signs (12):** the shared invariant is the *sign* — one cognitive function focused
  through one Enneagram center. 3 decans each.
- **Planetary (7):** the shared invariant is the *planet's rulership* — the pairing (or, for
  the luminaries, the single set) of the decans in the signs it rules. 6 decans (5 planets)
  or 3 decans (Sun, Moon).
