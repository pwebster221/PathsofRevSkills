## The four arcs (fixed, entailed)

**The throne names the suit:** the introverted sovereign sits at the season's fixed sign, so the fixed sign's element is the Page's element. Attitude by modality: **fixed signs host introverted parts; cardinal and mutable host extraverted.** Walk: **nocturnal Pages (Pentacles, Chalices) walk with the year** — the walking part keeps the mutable passing door; **diurnal Pages (Wands, Swords) walk against it** — the walking part keeps the cardinal gate. The far part keeps the door behind. The dark part — the other introverted one — owns no ground in the arc.

**Why only this mapping holds (the entailment, compressed).** Each quadrant's three signs carry three elements; the missing fourth is the tertiary — and the Tertiary Gap Lemma requires the quadrant to contain the dominant, auxiliary, and inferior elements only. Winter (Cap/Aqu/Pis = Earth/Air/Water) holds no Fire, so its Page's absent part is Intuition → dominant at fixed Aquarius = inward Thinking → Swords. Autumn holds no Earth → absent Sensing, dominant at Scorpio = inward Feeling → Chalices (the canon's own worked example: water→feeling, fire→intuition, air→thinking, sensing dark). Summer holds no Air → Wands; Spring holds no Water → Pentacles. Cross-checks that all pass only under this mapping: the walk direction seats every auxiliary correctly (nocturnal→mutable, diurnal→cardinal, inferior opposite); sister pairs exchange dominant↔tertiary (Pentacles↔Chalices, Wands↔Swords) and sit opposite on the wheel (Spring↔Autumn, Summer↔Winter); seasons alternate sect around the year, so **competition (adjacent) always crosses polarity and the sister challenge always runs within it**.

## Stations, occupants, and the gap

Chart-relative, whole-sign throughout. Each station resolves to the house its sign occupies (plain-language list below); occupants are that sign's placements from the D1 population (planets, luminaries, angles, both node measures, both Liliths, Chiron, comets, asteroids, lots — stars excluded, as in D2), name + essential nature; volume felt (banding as D2: empty 0 · thin 1–2 · peopled 3+ without planetary majority · crowded 5+ with planetary majority).

**The gap (mandatory, never blank):** the dark part points at the **sister's throne** — the house where the sister's fixed sign falls — seated like a station, occupied or vacant. Consequence, always: the querent's four throne-houses are **double-booked** — one Page's enthroned sovereign and her challenger's dark part, the same kind of mind twice. Gap targets: Pentacles→Scorpio's house · Wands→Aquarius's · Chalices→Taurus's · Swords→Leo's.

## The relations — Option A feed (inter-Page)

Currency-blind: a Page's political strength is her sovereign element's **whole A pole total** from D2. Her terrain, in a rival's or sister's payload, is that element's full company (names + natures), felt not counted — company felt-volumes carry over from D2. Each Page holds exactly three relations: **competes** with the two adjacent seasons (the rival "walks the other way" — always true, since seasons alternate sect) and is **opposed** by the sister across the wheel. A/B resolve in use, without ranking: **A governs inter-Page relations, B governs intra-Page constitution.**
