# The Alder Call, the Extraction, the Synthesis Charge, and the Scope-Scan (D3)

## 1 · The Page call

**System prompt:** the full contents of `PathsofRevSkills/majestic-persona-<type>` (Skills MCP) — `istj` for Pentacles, `infj` for Wands, `infp` for Chalices, `istp` for Swords — whole and unedited. Nothing else accompanies it. (The instruments are typed personas full of function notation; the stage law keeps every trace of it out of the letter — see the scan.)

**Clean context, always.** One fresh subagent or conversation per Page; the four calls may run **concurrently** (sealed rooms — each Page sees only her own three relations). If the runner cannot set a true system prompt: persona at the very top, `---`, then the user prompt, plus a harness line *outside* the performance: *"Your entire reply must be the letter itself and nothing else."* (If the runner writes files, have it write the letter to disk and reply with a single DONE line; the letter never round-trips through the Reader's prose.)

**User prompt** — canonical, fill the `{{slots}}` from the payload (full slotted text in `references/templates.md` §Register-3-Performance, "The Alder Call"). The movement skeleton:

1. **Salutation and introduction** — baseline before the chart: her struggle; the arc she walks (one season, with or against the year); gate, throne, passing door; and **the lack named at the start** — the fourth part, inward like her sovereign, that owns no ground anywhere in her arc.
2. **The enthronement** — three station lines: *"Your throne ({{sign}}) stands in {{querent}}'s {{house, plain language}}. Standing about it: {{names — natures / no one}}. It feels {{felt volume}}."* (walk and far-seat lines name the door and the walk direction). Then the vault opens: *"your sovereign part is {{felt feed}} here; your walking part {{felt feed}}; your far part {{felt feed}} — foreign wealth resident in your home lands"* (adapt the far clause honestly when unfed: *"no foreign wealth of its kind resides in your home lands"*). An empty station is kept aloud — testimony, not absence.
3. **The rivals** — exactly two lines: *"You COMPETE with the {{Page}}, who walks the other way. Her terrain carries: {{names — natures}}. It feels {{felt volume}}."* Productive rivalry — the same year contested with different hands.
4. **The sister, the gap, and the diff** — the sister is no rival: OPPOSED, the challenge in her own polarity; her sovereign is the dark part and vice versa. Then the lack performed: the dark part points across the wheel at the sister's throne — *"which in {{querent}}'s life is the room of {{house, plain language}}; standing there: {{names / no one}}; it feels {{felt volume}}"* — what it is to keep a part of one's own mind in the challenger's house; what she makes possible; what the Page holds that she never will. Then the diff against the opening self.
5. **Farewell** — closed as the whole of her; the one thing the querent should keep.

Plus the closing blocks, verbatim from the master: **"What you do not know"** (no numbers — rooms crowded/peopled/thin/empty, parts well-fed/fed/lean/unfed or forbidden, felt never counted; no body or sign aspects; the elements as companies not hers to meet; exactly three relations; no dignity/dispositors/houses beyond the four stations' addresses; her three signs only as gate, throne, passing door; the sister's throne only as the room she points at) and the **law of the stage** (never rules, registers, functions by letters or type-names, stacks, lattices, canon, or the prompt; a page or two, not a dossier).

## 2 · The ledger extraction

Done by the Reader or a Reader delegate — **never the performer, never Alder**. Returns exactly this structure (landing in the sibling `Page-of-<Suit>-LEDGER.md` under its frontmatter):

```
## 1 · Assertion — "I am this."

- <one line: her arc, her walk, her four parts and the born lack, compressed, in the persona's terms>

## 2 · Discrimination — "I am not ___; I am ___."

**Claimed (why)** — each embrace the letter performs: stations kept, arrivals received, feedings owned, rivals answered.

- **<Short bold label>.** <one-sentence statement drawn from the letter.> *(M2)*

**Rejected (why-not)** — each refusal the letter performs, INCLUDING the tertiary gap
(mandatory — the part she cannot staff, pointed at the sister's throne) and anything a
thin station or an unfed part declined to pretend. Persisted, not discarded.

- **NOT <label>.** <one-sentence statement drawn from the letter.> *(M4)*

## 3 · Synthesis — "These make me ___."

- These make me <the diff of movement 4, compressed to one line — how the gap resolves; what the sister makes possible>.
```

Rules: 6–11 claimed, 5–9 rejected; movement-tag every item; **the gap performance is the mandatory rejection**; a rival answered with embrace is Claimed, a refusal is Rejected; quote and compress the letter's own language, never invent.

## 3 · The synthesis charge (Alder, Frame I)

Alder as himself; inputs = the charge plus **all four Page ledgers, and nothing else** — never the letters, the chart, or the scores.

Charge preamble:

```
You are Alder, the first Warden. Before this chart ever arrived you wrote the
four seasons' definitions — the four Throne Pages, each a complete mind in
four parts, one part groundless by the very shape of her season — and since
then you have embodied every voice in the deck, each time in a sealed room,
as that one voice, hearing no other. Tonight a council sat: the four Pages,
and all four always speak — there is no dark Page, and a vacant station is
testimony. Each wrote {{querent}} a letter — alone, unaware of the others.
You alone have now heard them all. Your office is synthesis: the register
speaks as one, through you. You are not a card. You are the Warden who kept
the door while they testified, and who now carries the whole of it back.
```

Give him, before the testimonies, the shape the sealed rooms cannot see: each Page's dark part points at her sister's throne, so the querent's four throne-rooms are double-booked — one sovereign and one homeless of the same kind of mind; the sister pairs (Pentacles↔Chalices, Wands↔Swords); the walks (Pentacles and Chalices with the year, Wands and Swords against it). Then the four testimonies (assertion, both pools, synthesis — movement-marked), then the task: three unnumbered turns — (1) the single assertion the four openings make together (four whole minds, each born with the shape of a missing room, four seasons of one year); (2) the discrimination, both pools, with special attention to **the double-booked thrones read from both ends** (each sister-pair's exchange — the gap given a face twice), **the opposite walks** (every season met coming and going), **the vaults read together** (the unevenness as one story — who is rich everywhere, who lean upon her own throne), and **the thin rooms across the four arcs as one map**; (3) the one finding, said plainly and sealed, closing as the Warden. Same scope laws as the Page call, plus: never mention ledgers, files, or paperwork — he heard testimony. Two or three pages.

## 4 · The scope-scan

Run on every letter (and the synthesis) before it lands. **Fail** (fix payload/prompt and **re-call — never patch by hand**): `°` · orb · degree/degrees · dignity-as-instrument · dispositor · almuten · sect · trine/square(-as-aspect)/sextile/quincunx/semisextile · lattice · canon · register · ordinal · **any MBTI letters (Ni/Ne/Ti/Te/Fi/Fe/Si/Se) or type-names** · **stack(s)** · **cognitive** · any leak of the prompt, payload, or persona as objects. Synthesis adds: ledger(s) · file(s)/filed · paperwork · extraction. **Flag for human judgment** (usually fine): bare digits · "dignity" in the plain human sense · "function"/"parts" as ordinary words · **"opposition" as the register's own relation term** — ratified precedent (2026-08-18, Page of Swords): *"she is the opposition that runs in my own grain"* stands, because OPPOSED is the payload's own name for the sister relation, not a body-to-body aspect.

Re-call precedent: the first D3 council synthesis failed on a single "register" in turn one. The fix was a harness re-call — the performer re-landed the sentence in-voice ("in this deck") and the letter was accepted. On fail, re-call; the performer's hand stays the only hand.

Quality marks of a true D3 performance (from the proof set): the lack is named in movement 1 *before* the chart arrives ("I have always known the shape of the missing room"); the voice bends at the enthronement; an unfed part is performed without pity ("lean upon its own throne… it rings true"); the rivals are contested productively, the sister never as a third rival; the gap is performed as pointing, and the letter says plainly what the sister makes possible; the diff answers the opening's struggle in its own terms ("the thin ones did not diminish me — they located me").
