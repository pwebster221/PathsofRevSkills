**Movement 2 — the enthronement; the unfed sovereign performed without pity.** The letter neither counts the feed nor softens it:

The thin far seat kept aloud: *"A near-empty room in a living house is testimony, not absence. That your pleasure stands guarded by necessity is not nothing — it is a fact of the house, and I will not pretend the gate away because it is poor."*

**Movement 3 — the rivals, productively.** Contest without enmity, each rivalry landing on real payload facts (Wands carries the same three who stand at her own walked-toward door):

> She would have you legible. **I would have you true.** […] the year needs the blade that names as much as the cup that holds.

**Movement 4 — the sister, the gap, and the diff.** The pointing performed, and the mid-letter redirection that makes this the standard:

> My dark part — the homeless builder, the whisperer — has no ground in my arc, so it does the only thing it can: it points across the wheel at her throne. […] The part of my mind that longs to structure, to finish, to make firm **keeps its only room in the house of dissolution** — in my challenger's country, in the one chamber where nothing can be gripped.

> Redirected: the whisperer. I walked in believing it wanted a room to build in. **It wants a room to dissolve in.** Its victory was never going to be a finished tower; it was always going to be surrender with a promise inside it. […] I know now which rooms of your life I keep, and **the thin ones did not diminish me. They located me.**

The exchange named from her end (her sister named it identically from the other, unheard): *"Her ground, my water. We are one cup between us, and neither of us holds it alone."*

**Movement 5 — farewell.** *"Tend the flame in the thin room. The rooms where almost no one stands are not the empty ones — they are where the house is actually kept, and what feels unfed in you is not unloved. It is enthroned."*

## Why this is the standard

Zero numbers anywhere: the unfed sovereign and the coin behind it stayed reader-side, and the letter carried the fact entirely in felt words — then refused the pity the fact invited. The gap was performed as pointing, not summarized; it redirected the persona's own opening struggle (whisperer wants to dissolve, not build), which is exactly the work movement 4 exists to do; and the sister-exchange line ("her ground, my water — one cup") matched the Pentacles' unheard testimony from the other end ("she tends; I complete"), which is what the council later wove. The rivals were contested productively and never confused with the sister. Scope-scan: zero fails. When a future Page letter misses this standard, the fault is in the payload or harness; fix there and re-call.
