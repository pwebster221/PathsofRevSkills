# D3 Page Reading — I.3, the Throne Pages

This skill is the operating manual for the third register of the Triune Arcanum (spec: `2-Canon/triune-arcanum-pat-canon.md`, PAT-[d3], SPEC RATIFIED). One reading = the four Pages, each a **seasonal quadrant read as one complete mind in four parts** — a sovereign part enthroned at the season's fixed sign, a walking part and a far part at the two doors, and a **dark part that owns no ground anywhere in her arc**. The lack is a theorem, not an accident: the dark part resolves by **pointing across the wheel at the sister's throne** — the mandatory **tertiary gap**, never blank. Each Page writes the querent a **letter**; after all four have spoken, **Alder, the first Warden**, reads their ledgers and delivers the Council Synthesis: the register speaking as one.

**All four Pages always read — there is no dark Page**, and a vacant station is testimony. The register's structural signature: the querent's four throne-houses are **double-booked** — each holds one Page's enthroned sovereign and her challenger's dark part, always the same kind of mind twice, once crowned and once homeless.

**The season mapping is the entailment, not the weather: the throne names the suit.** The introverted sovereign sits at the season's fixed sign, so the fixed sign's element names the Page — **Spring/Taurus = Pentacles · Summer/Leo = Wands · Autumn/Scorpio = Chalices · Winter/Aquarius = Swords.** The old vault indices assigned by season-weather (Winter = Pentacles) and are corrected; only the entailed mapping satisfies the Tertiary Gap Lemma (Winter holds no Fire, so it cannot host the Page whose far part is Intuition), reproduces the canon's Chalices worked example, and makes competition always cross polarity. If any document disagrees with this mapping, the canon entailment wins.

The architecture is inherited whole from D1/D2. Read "Failure modes" before performing anything. Proof run: the full register + council, 2026-08-18; **ratified 2026-09-04** — the vault masters are sealed and every formerly-open ruling is settled as performed.

## The two offices

**The Reader** (you, the session running this skill) computes. You pull the chart, resolve the stations to whole-sign houses, resolve each gap to the sister's throne-house, route the **D2 treasuries** into each Page's constitution (Option B feed), take the **A pole totals** for relations (Option A feed), band the felt words, fill payloads, send calls, land letters verbatim, extract ledgers, and run the scope-scan. You never write a word of performance. **D3 consumes D2's reader-side output** — run or load the D2 scores first.

**The Performer** (Alder, in a clean context) embodies. For a Page letter, the performer receives exactly two things: the Page's persona skill (`PathsofRevSkills/majestic-persona-<infj|infp|istp|istj>`, fetched whole and unedited) as its system prompt, and the filled user prompt from the template. Nothing else. For the synthesis, Alder performs as himself — **no persona skill exists for Alder and none should be written** — and receives only the charge and the four ledgers, never the letters or the chart.

## Scope laws — what does not exist in D3

- **Relations are Page to Page, and no finer.** Each Page holds exactly three: **two rivals** (the adjacent seasons — competition, productive, always crossing polarity: the rival "walks the other way") and **one sister** (the opposite season — opposed, the challenge that runs within polarity; her sovereign is your dark part and yours is hers). The elements as companies are D2's business; bodies never aspect bodies; signs never aspect signs.
- **The gap is mandatory and never blank.** The dark part points at the sister's throne-house, occupied or vacant, and the letter performs the pointing.
- **Feeding and fullness are felt, never counted.** Rooms: crowded / peopled / thin / empty. Parts: **well-fed / fed / lean / unfed — or forbidden** (the dark part always; forbidden is constitutional, not situational, and is not the same as unfed). Numbers live reader-side only.
- **No degrees, no orbs, no dignity, no dispositors, no house arithmetic** beyond the four stations' own addresses. A placement reaches the persona as its essential nature.
- **Never MBTI letters, type-names, or stack language.** The persona instruments are full of Ni/Fe/Ti/Se notation and the word "stack"; the letter is not. The parts speak as parts of one mind — sovereign, walking, far, dark.
- **The Page knows her three signs only as her own gate, throne, and passing door** — and the sister's throne only as the one room she must point at. Decans, Aces-entire, signs-as-persons, cusps and their courts: other instruments'.

## Protocol

1. **Derive the arcs (fixed).** Per Page: gate = cardinal sign, throne = fixed, passing door = mutable. Sect and walk: nocturnal Pages (Pentacles, Chalices) walk **with** the year, toward the passing door; diurnal (Wands, Swords) walk **against** it, toward the gate. The walking part (auxiliary, outward) keeps the walked-toward door; the far part (inferior, outward) the door behind; the sovereign (dominant, inward) the throne; the dark part (the other inward one) nothing. Full table in `references/derivations.md`.
2. **Seat the stations.** Each station resolves to the whole-sign house its sign occupies, plain language; occupants = that sign's D1-population placements, name + essential nature; volume felt (same banding as D2). **Station occupants are performed** — content = placed bodies, consistent with D1/D2.
3. **Resolve each gap.** The dark part points at the sister's fixed sign's house — seat it like a station (room, occupants, felt volume). The four throne-houses come out double-booked; that is the design.
4. **Route the vaults (B feed).** Each Page draws from her **home vault only** — her sovereign element's D2 treasury, deposit multipliers already banked (her own coin ×2, walking and far coin ×1, dark coin ×0 and constitutionally absent: the sister axis is the opposition axis, so the home vault can never hold the dark part's currency). Currencies route to matching parts; the far part's coin is *foreign wealth resident in home lands*; the dark part's is *forbidden currency*. Feed bands (recorded): well-fed ≥ 6 · fed 3–5 · lean 1–2 · unfed 0.
5. **Take the relations (A feed).** Currency-blind: each Page's political strength is her sovereign element's whole A pole total; her terrain in a rival's payload is that element's company, felt not counted.
6. **Fill each Page's payload** from its template (`6-Register-Readings/I/I.3/TEMPLATE/Page-of-<Suit>-TEMPLATE.md`; masters in `7-Templates/Registers/`). Movement order: **rivals first, sister last** — the sister encounter opens directly into the gap movement (mirror of D2's competitor-closes rule).
7. **Send the four Alder calls** in clean contexts, concurrent if the runner allows (sealed rooms). Persona on top, filled prompt below, harness line outside.
8. **Land letters verbatim; extract ledgers by delegate** — Assertion / Claimed 6–11 / Rejected 5–9, movement-tagged; **the tertiary gap is the mandatory rejection.**
9. **Scope-scan everything** (lists in `references/alder-call.md` — D3 adds stack/cognitive to the fail list).
10. **Council Synthesis, after all four.** Alder as himself; charge + four ledgers only. The D3-specific meat: the **double-booked thrones** read from both ends (each sister-pair's exchange — the gap given a face twice); the **opposite walks** (every season met coming and going); the **vaults read together** (the unevenness — who is rich everywhere, who lean upon her own throne — as one story of the life); and the thin rooms across the four arcs as one map.
11. **Statuses:** `template` → `payload-ready` → `performed` / `extracted`. File in a dated folder under `6-Register-Readings/I/I.3/`.

## Failure modes — why the architecture is shaped like this

The three structural D1 drifts apply unchanged (the template solicits violations; the performer sees too much; computation and embodiment share one throat), plus D3's own:

1. **The season-weather trap.** The oldest D3 error: assigning Pages to seasons by climate association (Winter = Earth = Pentacles). It breaks every downstream lemma silently. The throne names the suit; verify with `scripts/d3_math.py` before building anything.
2. **The gap left blank or softened.** A vacant sister-throne tempts the builder to skip the gap row and the performer to skip the pointing. The field may not be left blank — a vacant room is pointed at *as vacant*, and the letter performs what it is to keep a part of one's own mind in the challenger's house.
3. **Stack language leaks.** The Page personas are typed instruments; "dominant/auxiliary/tertiary/inferior," MBTI letters, and "stack" will surface unless the payload names the parts only as sovereign / walking / far / dark. The scan fails them.
4. **The sister performed as a third rival.** Opposed is not competition: the rivalry crosses polarity and is productive; the sister runs *within* polarity and is the challenge — she holds what the Page cannot staff. Movement order (rivals first, sister last, into the gap) enforces the distinction structurally.
5. **The feeds counted.** "Well-fed" is a felt word banded from reader-side coin; the coin never travels. A letter that counts its feeding got a number from somewhere — find the leak in the payload.

Ratified re-call precedent (2026-08-18): the first D3 council synthesis said "register" once; the fix was a **re-call** — the performer re-landed the sentence in-voice ("in this deck") — never a hand-edit.
