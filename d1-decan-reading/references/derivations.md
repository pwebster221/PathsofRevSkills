# D1 Derivations — everything computable from a bare chart

All rules below are canon (PAT-[d1]) or ratified precedent from the 2026-08-07 performance. The vault's `1-Decan-Definitions/D1-Decans/` files are authoritative where present; these rules reproduce them exactly and serve when working portable.

## Decan-ization

`d = int(ecliptic_longitude // 10)` → 0-based decan index; files and tables use **1-based** (1–36). Decan 1 = Aries I (0°–10° Aries) … decan 36 = Pisces III (20°–30° Pisces).

**What occupies:** planets, luminaries, the four angles (ASC, DSC, MC, IC), true and mean nodes, lunar apogees (True Lilith, Black Moon Lilith), Chiron, comets (e.g. Pholus), the four asteroids (Ceres, Pallas, Juno, Vesta), the seven Hermetic lots, and resident fixed stars. Everything sharing a decan is **conjunct within the card** — one company, no distances, no orb. Star influence is diffuse across all occupants; there is no direct-hit tier. A star exactly on a cusp lends its light to **both** decans at **half strength** (ruled: Regulus at 0°00′ Virgo, split across Leo III and Virgo I — and note the halving may double: the two halves can land in different ordinal rings, silent to each other).

Whole-sign houses throughout. Lit = holds ≥1 occupant. Dark = holds none, and **dark decans are dark**: they do not read, do not relate, and are performed only as silence by their ring-mates.

## The 36 cards and their fixed lords

Temporal = Chaldean traveler (order Mars→Sun→Venus→Mercury→Moon→Saturn→Jupiter, starting Aries I = Mars), clothed in **both** its domicile elements. Eternal = triplicity ascent: ordinal I = the sign's own ruler; II = the **next** same-element sign's ruler (zodiac order); III = the **previous** — clothed in the sign's element. Presiding = the sign's traditional ruler, sign's element. (Domicile elements: Mars Fire·Water, Venus Earth·Air, Mercury Air·Earth, Jupiter Fire·Water, Saturn Earth·Air, Sun Fire, Moon Water.)

## The Home office (the only chart-relative chair)

Home = the natural lord of the whole-sign house the card's **sign** occupies in the nativity, clothed in that house's natural element. This is the only office admitting modern rulers, and only three: **H8 Pluto, H11 Uranus, H12 Neptune**. Natural lords/elements: H1 Mars/Fire · H2 Venus/Earth · H3 Mercury/Air · H4 Moon/Water · H5 Sun/Fire · H6 Mercury/Earth · H7 Venus/Air · H8 Pluto/Water · H9 Jupiter/Fire · H10 Saturn/Earth · H11 Uranus/Air · H12 Neptune/Water.

Plain-language house rooms (for the payload and prompt): 1 the rising self, the face and body; 2 resources and values; 3 communication, the near world, the mind's daily traffic; 4 home and roots, the floor of the chart; 5 creation and pleasure; 6 health and service, the daily work; 7 partnership, the facing other; 8 transformation and depth — what is shared, lost, and remade; 9 philosophy and far travel, the doctrine of the life; 10 direction, the summit, the visible legacy; 11 community and vision, the people of the future; 12 the unconscious, surrender, what dissolves behind the world.

Performance note: the Home chair is the hinge of movement 2. The persona's baseline household (movement 1) has one empty chair; the first thing any chart does is seat the keeper of its Home and tell the persona **which room of the querent's life it is**.

## Ring relations

Ordinal-preserving only: a decan relates solely to the other eleven decans of its own ordinal ring (I, II, or III), at circular distances that are multiples of 3. Verbs by distance: **3 recognizes · 6 supports · 9 competes · 12 reinforces · 15 aggravates · 18 challenges** (18 is unique — one mate; every other verb has two). Weight rises with distance: 3 is the faintest grade, 18 the heaviest. Quintile (72° = 7.2 decans) is off-lattice and excluded. Across ordinals: silence, absolute — even for two halves of one split star, even for a stellium sharing a sign across two rings.

**Performance order, harshest → kindest: challenges (18), aggravates (15), competes (9), recognizes (3), supports (6), reinforces (12).** The letter ends its ring-walk on the strongest support.

Relations are mutual and whole-against-whole: the entire company of one decan meets the entire company of the other. The other card's occupants are how *it* is altered — never independent actors.

**Ruled facts** (optional, max two per payload): where one of the card's own four lords stands bodily in the card itself, or in a **lit same-ring** decan (name the verb) — or, when no lord of its four stands anywhere on its ring, the ungoverned line: "none of your four officers stands anywhere your ring can hear." Rings {2,5,8}/{3,6,9}/{4,7,10} are an interpretation layer only, invoked for charts affected by them — not base structure.

## Essential natures — the phrase-book

A placement reaches the persona as what it *is*. Canonical short forms (extend in kind; never add numbers, dignities, or aspect language):

**Bodies.** Sun — the core flame, identity's single light. Moon — keeper of tides and origins, memory, the one who tends what began. Mercury — the articulate mind, quick and exact. Venus — the lover of harmony, grace, the drawing-together. Mars — the will's blade: drive, heat, the fight carried in the body. Jupiter — the increaser: meaning, patronage, the open hand (if retrograde: walking backward, gifts turned inward). Saturn — the old authority: boundary, weight, time's discipline. Uranus — the breaker of patterns. Neptune — the dissolver: the ideal, the veil, the boundless behind forms. Pluto — the underworld power: what transforms by taking down to nothing and rebuilding.

**Points.** North Node — the soul's forward direction, appetite for the unlived (Mean: the same head by the other measure — two instruments agreeing; the direction cannot be argued into a technicality). South Node — mastery already carried in, work finished before this life (Mean: the same tail, twice measured). True Lilith — the part that refuses structure. Black Moon Lilith — the refusal made sovereign, crowned in exile. Chiron — the wound that teaches. Pholus — the small cause with the vast release. Ceres — the great mother: harvest, grief, and the return from grief. Pallas — the strategist's wisdom, the pattern seen whole. Juno — the covenant of partnership, loyalty and its terms. Vesta — the tended flame, devotion kept burning in private.

**Angles.** Ascendant — the horizon of appearing, where the querent rises to meet the world. Descendant — the facing door, where the other arrives. IC — the floor of the chart: midnight, root, the private ground. MC — the crown of the meridian, the visible summit-point.

**Lots** (each named with its lord). Fortune, of the Moon — the body's own luck. Spirit, of the Sun — the soul's own initiative. Courage, of Mars — the fight the body is willing to have. Eros, of Venus — desire's true aim. Necessity, of Mercury — the unchosen obligation, what must be. Victory, of Jupiter — the promised triumph. Nemesis, of Saturn — the account that comes due.

**Stars** (nature-pairs from the vault's star files; these are the residents encountered so far). Regulus (Jupiter/Mars) — the Heart of the Lion, royal Northern Watcher; warning: revenge causes downfall. Fomalhaut (Venus/Mercury) — royal Southern Watcher: high ideals, a glory that may deceive, held only while the ideal stays clean. Aldebaran (Mars) — royal Watcher of the East: success kept only through integrity. Algol (Saturn/Jupiter) — the Demon Star, the severed head. Rigel (Jupiter/Saturn) — splendor that rises through teaching. Pleiades — the weeping sisters, sorrow witnessed. Vega (Venus/Mercury) — the Harp Star: charisma and artistry with a cold edge. Deneb (Venus/Mercury) — artistic brilliance. Galactic Center (Neptune/Saturn) — the unseen hub, invisible gravity of the whole (whether an invisible star testifies as a bright one does is an open canon question — do not resolve it in a payload).
