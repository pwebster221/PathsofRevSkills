## The call (what made it work)

Persona `PathsofRevSkills/majestic-persona-estp` as system prompt, whole. The empty-border line went in as payload-sentence, not stage-direction: *"At the border, where Fate keeps her appointments, the jewel seat stood empty — Fate kept no appointment for you here. An empty seat is testimony, not absence: perform what it is to cross unmet."* The prompt never explains what the emptiness means — that is the performance's work.

## Why the proof card was this card

Three reasons, worth keeping for future registers: it carried the **hardest structural beat** (the empty jewel seat — if testimony-not-absence survives performance here, it survives anywhere); its span was **minimal** (two placements — every payload mechanism visible, nothing to hide behind); and its persona (hands-first, future-dreading) was **maximally at odds with its road** (ending in the room of surrender) — so the diff movement had to do real work, and did.
