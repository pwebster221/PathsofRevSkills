# The Alder Call, the Extraction, the Relay Charge, and the Scope-Scan (D4)

## 1 · The court call

**System prompt:** the full contents of `PathsofRevSkills/majestic-persona-<type>` (Skills MCP) — roster in `derivations.md` §7 — whole and unedited. Nothing else accompanies it. (The instruments are typed personas full of function notation; the stage law keeps every trace of it out of the letter — see the scan.)

**Clean context, always.** One fresh subagent or conversation per court; the twelve calls may run **concurrently** (sealed rooms — each court sees only its own seven relations). Two ratified harness patterns:

- **Inline** (D1–D3 pattern): persona at the very top, `---`, the filled user prompt, `---`, then a harness line *outside* the performance: write the letter to disk, reply with a single DONE.
- **In-room fetch** (ratified 2026-09-04): the performer, instructed as harness, (1) fetches its own persona skill by exact name and becomes it, (2) reads its filled call-prompt file as the user's message, (3) writes the letter to disk, (4) replies DONE — *"Do not read any other files, do not use any other tools, and do not let anything in this harness note enter the letter."* Same sealed contents, no transcription risk. Either pattern is valid; nothing else may enter the room.

**User prompt** — canonical, fill the `{{slots}}` from the payload (full slotted text in `references/templates.md` §Register-4-Performance, "The Alder Call"). The five-movement skeleton:

1. **Salutation and introduction** — baseline before the chart: who the traveler is, their struggle, and the crossing they are — the road out of {leaving} into {entering}, the two countries by function and chamber, the suit narrative, the rank narrative; two countries and three moments, none yet belonging to a life. *The reader must know you before anything bends you.*
2. **The road** — the chart arrives and the voice bends: out of the querent's {leaving room} across the border into their {entering room}; then the three station lines in journey order — departure (what was given, prepared, or fought), border (*"where Fate keeps her appointments"* — or the jewel seat empty: *"Fate kept no appointment for you here. An empty seat is testimony, not absence: perform what it is to cross unmet"*), road's end (the wage, sealed until arrival). *Receive each by what it is… say what it is to leave one room of the querent's life and arrive in another.*
3. **The road's company** — seven of eleven, in order from the crossroads to the mirror: two CHALLENGES (rank-mates on the perpendicular crossroads), PRECEDES (what they finish, you inherit), SUCCEEDS (what you finish, they inherit), two EMPOWERS (suit-kin at other gates), COMPLEMENTS closing (across the whole wheel, the crossing mirrored — the deepest encounter ends the movement). Each line: their crossing, their road's cargo as natures, its felt volume.
4. **The diff** — the traveler beside the one who set out: what the chart answered, inflamed, or redirected; the change named plainly.
5. **Farewell** — from the far country, as one who has crossed; the one thing the querent should keep.

Plus the closing blocks, verbatim from the master: **"What you do not know"** (no numbers — a moment is crowded/peopled/thin/empty and an empty seat is testimony; no body or sign aspects — relations are court to court and exactly seven, the map's edge not a wound; no dignity/dispositors/houses beyond the two countries' rooms; no thirty-six little rooms, functions entire, seasons, thrones, or signs as persons — two countries and three moments of one road; a placement arrives as what it essentially is) and the **law of the stage** (never rules, registers, functions by letters or type-names, stacks, lattices, canon, or the prompt; a page or two, not a dossier).

## 2 · The ledger extraction

Done by the Reader or a Reader delegate — **never the performer, never Alder**. The delegate may read the court's call-prompt file first *for structure only* (movement map, station and companion names), then the letter; it writes the ledger and nothing else. Structure (landing in the sibling `<Card>-LEDGER.md` under its frontmatter):

```
## 1 · Assertion — "I am this."

- <one line: the traveler's claim — the crossing they are, the two countries, the road's shape, compressed, in the persona's terms>

## 2 · Discrimination — "I am not ___; I am ___."

**Claimed (why)** — each embrace the letter performs: stations received in road-order, empty seats kept aloud, company answered. One line apiece, movement-tagged *(M2)* / *(M3)* / *(M4)*.

- **<Short bold label>.** <one-sentence statement drawn from the letter.> *(M2)*

**Rejected (why-not)** — each refusal the letter performs, including what an empty jewel seat declined to pretend and any road refused on the company walk. Persisted, not discarded.

- **NOT <label>.** <one-sentence statement drawn from the letter.> *(M2)*

## 3 · Synthesis — "These make me ___."

- These make me <the diff of movement 4, compressed to one line — how the placements resolve the crossing>.
```

Rules: 6–11 claimed, 5–9 rejected; movement-tag every item; an empty-station performance belongs wherever the letter performs it (an embrace of crossing unmet is Claimed, a refusal to pretend company is Rejected — often both, as separate items); an answered companion who embraces is Claimed, a refusal is Rejected; quote and compress the letter's own language, never invent.

## 3 · The relay charge (Alder, Frame I)

Alder as himself; inputs = the charge plus **all twelve ledgers, in relay order around the wheel, and nothing else** — never the letters, the chart, or the scores. (Relay order: Queen of Wands, Knight of Pentacles, King of Swords, Queen of Chalices, Knight of Wands, King of Pentacles, Queen of Swords, Knight of Chalices, King of Wands, Queen of Pentacles, Knight of Swords, King of Chalices.)

Charge preamble:

```
You are Alder, the first Warden. Before this chart ever arrived you wrote the
twelve cusps' definitions — the Court of Change, the senior court: twelve
travelers, each the guardian of one crossing between two signs, each a whole
personality walking the one road it was made for. And since then you have
embodied every voice in the deck, each time in a sealed room, as that one
voice, hearing no other. Tonight a council sat: the twelve courts, and all
twelve always travel — there is no dark court, and an empty seat is testimony.
Each wrote {{querent}} a letter — alone, unaware of the others. You alone have
now heard them all. Your office is synthesis: this suite of the deck speaks as
one, through you. You are not a card. You are the Warden who kept the door
while they testified, and who now carries the whole of it back.
```

Give him, before the testimonies, the shape the sealed rooms cannot see: **the relay** (the twelve roads tile the whole sky exactly; each road's end lies at the next road's departure; what each finishes, the next inherits — no traveler knows this, the Warden does); **the three offices** (the rank crossings, each performed four times; each suit's elemental crossing performed three times); **the empty seats** of this chart, named; **the crowded gate against the thin roads**, named; **the mirrors** (seven of eleven seen; cannot-see is anatomy; the six complement pairs walk each other's crossing mirrored). Then the twelve testimonies, then the task: three unnumbered turns — (1) the single assertion the twelve openings make together (twelve whole travelers, one relay, the whole round of one life crossed room to room); (2) the discrimination, both pools, with special attention to **the relay read as handoffs** (traced once around the whole wheel through the querent's rooms), **the empty seats as one testimony**, **the crowded gate against the thin roads** (the unevenness as one story of the life), and **the mirrors met from both ends** (testimonies that answer each other sealed and apart); (3) the one finding, said plainly and sealed, closing as the Warden. Same scope laws as the court call, plus: never mention ledgers, files, or paperwork — he heard testimony. Two or three pages.

## 4 · The scope-scan

Run on every letter (and the synthesis) before it lands. **Fail** (fix payload/prompt and **re-call — never patch by hand**): `°` · orb · degree/degrees · dispositor · almuten · sect · trine/square(-as-aspect)/sextile/quincunx/semisextile · lattice · canon · register · **dignity as an instrument** · **decan(s)** — the traveler must never learn its composition · **any MBTI letters (Ni/Ne/Ti/Te/Fi/Fe/Si/Se) or type-names** · **stack(s)** · **cognitive** · dominant/auxiliary/tertiary/inferior-function language · payload · persona · prompt · any leak of the harness. Synthesis adds: ledger(s) · file(s)/filed · paperwork · extraction. **Flag for human judgment** (usually fine): bare digits in ordinary speech · "function" and "part(s)" as ordinary words (the phrase-book's own "the part that refuses structure" is payload language and stands) · "square"/"opposition" in plain human senses.

Re-call precedent (D3, 2026-08-18, stands for D4): on a fail, the fix is a harness **re-call** — the performer re-lands the sentence in-voice; the performer's hand stays the only hand. The 2026-09-04 D4 run needed none: twelve letters and the synthesis, zero fails.

Quality marks of a true D4 performance (from the ratified run): the struggle is named in movement 1 *before* the chart arrives; the voice bends at the road; an empty jewel seat is crossed without dressing it ("No test, no toll, no face at the line"; "I bowed to it… and made the crossing itself the witness"); the succeeds-line is heard as inheritance ("I do the quiet part; he takes the bow"); the complement closes deepest, not loudest; the diff answers the opening's struggle in its own terms ("Most of what I dread is an empty chair").

## 5 · The Recognition Pass (after the scan)

Post-performance, reader-side, per the 2026-09-05 ruling — full protocol in `derivations.md` §8. In brief: score **performed speech only** (letter passages cut at movements; ledger beats — never payload lines or prompts). Movement 1 against the card's own instrument is the **fidelity check** — a refused salutation is grounds for re-call, exactly like a scope-scan fail; the self-instrument across movements 1→2→4 is the **bend profile**; targeted panels (self · seat-pip · seat-reader · neighbors) give the recognition constellation. All verdicts and why-nots stay in Reader's Scores; a why-not is another persona's first-person speech and never enters a letter.
