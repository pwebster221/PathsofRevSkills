# Major Arcana — Attribution Sheet

Source: `MajorArcana` nodes (Repository). This is the **authoritative inline
reference** for Major correspondences — read it directly at interpretation; no live
query needed. Pair it with `generative-principle.md` for the number layer.

## Classical attribution note

The nodes store **modern** planets for the three elemental Majors (Fool→Uranus,
Hanged Man→Neptune, Judgement→Pluto). In this system those three are the
**elemental** Majors — Fool = Air, Hanged Man = Water, Judgement = Fire (per
`elemental_primary`). Use the elemental reading; the modern planet is overlay only.
The seven planetary Majors are Magician (Mercury), High Priestess (Moon), Empress
(Venus), Wheel of Fortune (Jupiter), Tower (Mars), Sun (Sun), World (Saturn). All
other Majors are zodiacal (one sign each).

## Note: no Hermetic-principle layer

The Kybalion's seven "Hermetic Principles" are an early-1900s addition, not part of
the historical Hermetic corpus. That layer has been **retired and replaced by the
Generative Principle** (`generative-principle.md`), which is what colors each Major's
reading. Do not reintroduce a Hermetic-principle column.
