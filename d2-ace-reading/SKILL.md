# D2 Ace Reading — I.2, the Elemental Aces

This skill is the operating manual for the second register of the Triune Arcanum (spec: `2-Canon/triune-arcanum-pat-canon.md`, PAT-[d2]). One reading = the four Aces, each an element read as a **cognitive function** — Fire/Intuition (Wands), Water/Feeling (Chalices), Air/Thinking (Swords), Earth/Sensing (Pentacles). An Ace is the function **undifferentiated**: alpha and omega of its element, **two voices in one wisdom** — the outward source-voice and the inward end-voice. Each Ace keeps a three-roomed household (Heart/cardinal, Head/fixed, Gut/mutable), holds exactly **three outward relations** (two oblique, one competitive), and carries its conflict-axis **twin braided into its own flesh**, never met as an aspect. Each Ace writes the querent a **letter**; after all four have spoken, **Alder, the first Warden**, reads their ledgers and delivers the Council Synthesis: the register speaking as one.

**All four Aces always read — there is no dark Ace.** An empty room is testimony, not silence. This is the first structural difference from D1, and it changes the council: the synthesis never maps dark cards; it maps thin and crowded rooms across the four households instead.

The architecture is inherited whole from D1, where it was won after three generations of drift; the fix is structural, not disciplinary. Read "Failure modes" before performing anything. Ratified proof: the Ace of Pentacles, 2026-08-18 ("gorgeous" — Paul); full register + council completed and ratified the same day.

## The two offices

Every reading involves exactly two roles, and they must never share a context.

**The Reader** (you, the session running this skill) computes. You pull the chart, group companies by element, resolve the rooms to whole-sign houses, classify the carriers, compute **both** scoring options reader-side, band the felt volumes, fill payloads, send calls, land letters verbatim, extract ledgers, and run the scope-scan. You never write a word of performance.

**The Performer** (Alder, in a clean context) embodies. For an Ace letter, the performer receives exactly two things: the function's persona skill (`PathsofRevSkills/majestic-persona-<intuition|feeling|thinking|sensing>`, fetched whole and unedited — the same genus instrument the Mars engine uses) as its system prompt, and the filled user prompt from the template. Nothing else. Not the chart, not the vault, not the scores, not other letters. For the synthesis, Alder performs as himself — **no persona skill exists for Alder and none should be written** — and receives only the charge and the four ledgers, never the letters or the chart.

The starvation is the whole defense: a performer who has never seen a number cannot count a room. What is not in the payload cannot leak.

## Scope laws — what does not exist in D2

These travel inside every call, and they bind the Reader's payload-building even harder than they bind the performer:

- **Aspects are element to element, and no finer.** Each Ace holds exactly three relations — **one competitive** (the zodiacal opposite: Fire↔Air, Earth↔Water; productive, not negative, not supportive; treasuries that can never hold each other's coin) and **two oblique** (cautious, neither supporting nor inimical). Sign-to-sign drama belongs to D5; body-to-body aspects to no register at all.
- **The twin is never an outward aspect.** The conflict-axis partner (Fire↔Water, Earth↔Air) is braided into the shared carriers. The discrimination beat is internal untangling — "I am not <twin>; I am <element>" — said of shared flesh, not of a stranger. The twin's *company* may be met (as one of the two obliques); the twin itself may not.
- **Volume is felt, never counted.** Rooms and companies are crowded / peopled / thin / empty — those four words and no others. Numbers live reader-side only: both scoring options A and B computed, unranked, the canon's deliberate tension held until freeze. They feed D3 and the Mars engine; they never enter a prompt or a letter.
- **No degrees, no orbs, no dignity, no sect, no dispositors, no house arithmetic** beyond the three rooms' own addresses. A placement reaches the persona as its **essential nature**.
- **Never name functions by MBTI letters or type-names.** The persona instruments are full of Se/Si/Ne/Ni notation; the letter is not. The two voices speak *as voices* — outward and inward — and the scaffolding disappears.
- **The decans, the signs as persons, and the courts are other registers' business.** The Ace knows its three signs only as its own three rooms.

## Protocol

1. **Pull and group.** Get the chart (Kairos or equivalent; whole-sign houses). The population is D1's, unchanged: planets, luminaries, the four angles, true and mean nodes, both Liliths, Chiron, comets, the four asteroids, and the seven Hermetic lots. Group every placement by the **element of its occupied sign** — four companies. Fixed stars do **not** join companies in this register: they enter only within a degree of a body, as a +2 score contact, and are otherwise absent.
2. **Resolve the rooms.** Each Ace's three rooms are fixed (Heart = its cardinal sign, Head = fixed, Gut = mutable; see `references/derivations.md`). Chart-relative: each room resolves to the whole-sign house its sign occupies, rendered in plain language. Occupants = that sign's company members, name + essential nature from the phrase-book. Both node measures count and are listed as one presence twice measured (ratified convention).
3. **Score reader-side, both options.** Option A (additive) and Option B (treasury, by currency) per `references/derivations.md` — computed, recorded in Reader's Scores, never sent. Band the felt volumes from occupant count with the scores as conscience (recorded banding: empty = 0 · thin = 1–2 · peopled = 3+ without planetary majority · crowded = 5+ with planetary majority, both options concurring).
4. **Fill each Ace's payload** from its template (`6-Register-Readings/I/I.2/TEMPLATE/Ace-of-<Suit>-TEMPLATE.md`; masters in `7-Templates/Registers/`). Rooms seated; the three aspect lines in ruled order — **obliques first (non-twin, then the twin's company), competitor last**; the carrier braid lines with each carrier's station given as element only.
5. **Send the four Alder calls** in clean contexts — one per Ace, concurrent if the runner allows (sealed rooms; each Ace sees only its own three relations, so the cards are independent by construction). Persona skill on top, filled user prompt below, harness line outside the performance.
6. **Land each letter verbatim.** Never edit a letter by hand. If it misses, fix the payload or prompt and call again.
7. **Extract each ledger** into the sibling `Ace-of-<Suit>-LEDGER.md` — by a Reader delegate, **never the performer**. Structure: Assertion / Claimed 6–11 / Rejected 5–9 (persisted!), movement-tagged, quoting the letter's own language. The twin-untangling lines belong in Rejected.
8. **Scope-scan every letter** before it lands. Fail and flag lists in `references/alder-call.md`.
9. **Council Synthesis, after all four.** Alder as himself; inputs = the charge + all four ledgers, nothing else. Three turns. The D2-specific meat: the twins untangled from **both ends of the same flesh** (Earth and Air each dividing Saturn–Venus–Mercury along the same seam; Fire and Water dividing Mars and Jupiter); the two rivalries answered from **both stalls of the market**; the **purest carriers followed home** to the rooms their bodies stand in; and the thin and crowded rooms across the four households read together as one map.
10. **Statuses:** `template` → `payload-ready` → `performed` (reading) / `extracted` (ledger). File everything in a dated folder under `6-Register-Readings/I/I.2/`.

## Failure modes — why the architecture is shaped like this

The three D1 drifts apply unchanged, and D2 adds two of its own:

1. **The template solicits violations.** A column named Degree/Dignity/Aspects *will* be filled. Fix: out-of-register data has no field to land in. The Reader's Scores section exists precisely so the numbers have somewhere to live that is never sent.
2. **The performer sees too much.** Fix: starve the performer. The A/B figures are the most dangerous cargo in this register — a persona handed a score will count its rooms. The scores inform the felt words; the felt words travel; the scores never do.
3. **Computation and embodiment share one throat.** Fix: two contexts, always — including "quick" single-Ace readings.
4. **The twin drifts outward.** Left unguarded, the conflict-axis partner becomes a fourth aspect and the discrimination beat collapses into rivalry. Fix: the payload's aspect table has exactly three rows, and the twin appears only inside the carrier braid lines. The twin's company is met; the twin is worn.
5. **Sign-mates leak upward.** The Ace's three signs invite sign-personality (D5's business) and its nine decans invite decan detail (D1's). Fix: the rooms are rooms — sign names appear only as room addresses, and no decan is ever named.

If a letter comes back counted, scored, or aspect-flavored, the cause is in the payload or the harness — not the persona. Diagnose there.
