# The Alder Call, the Extraction, the Synthesis Charge, and the Scope-Scan (D2)

## 1 · The Ace call

**System prompt:** the full contents of `PathsofRevSkills/majestic-persona-<function_lower>` (Skills MCP) — `intuition` for Wands, `feeling` for Chalices, `thinking` for Swords, `sensing` for Pentacles — whole and unedited, the same genus instrument the Mars engine uses for resonance scoring. Nothing else accompanies it.

**Clean context, always.** Run the call as a fresh subagent or fresh conversation, one per Ace. The four calls may run **concurrently** — the cards are sealed rooms, independent by construction; each Ace sees only its own three relations. If the runner cannot set a true system prompt, place the persona skill at the very top of the message, a `---` divider, then the user prompt — and add a harness line *outside* the performance: *"Your entire reply must be the letter itself and nothing else — no title before it, no commentary after it."* (If the runner writes files, have it write the letter to disk and reply with a single DONE line; the letter must never round-trip through the Reader's prose.)

**User prompt** — canonical, fill the `{{slots}}` from the payload (full slotted text lives in the master template, `references/templates.md` §Register-2-Performance, "The Alder Call"). The movement skeleton:

1. **Salutation and introduction** — baseline before the chart: the struggle, the two voices, the three unplaced rooms (Heart {{cardinal}}, Head {{fixed}}, Gut {{mutable}}).
2. **The arrivals** — three room lines: *"Your Heart room ({{sign}}) keeps {{querent}}'s {{house, plain language}}. Standing in it: {{names — essential natures / no one}}. It feels {{felt volume}}."* Each voice receives its own room, both the middle; an empty room is kept aloud — testimony, not absence.
3. **The others** — exactly three relation lines, in ruled order: OBLIQUE to the non-twin company, OBLIQUE to the twin's company, COMPETITIVE to the opposite — the most productive encounter closes the movement. Each line: the company's carriers as names + natures, and its felt volume.
4. **The twin, and the diff** — the carrier braid lines (*"Saturn is braided with your twin — you are his leading edge, Air his depth — and he stands in your own rooms…"*, stations as element names only), the untangling said of shared flesh — *"I am not {{twin}}; I am {{element}}"* — then the diff against the opening self.
5. **Farewell** — closed as one voice; the one thing the querent should keep.

Plus the closing blocks, verbatim from the master: **"What you do not know, because it does not exist for you"** (no numbers; element-to-element aspects only, exactly three; no dignity/sect/dispositors/houses beyond the three rooms' addresses; the thirty-six little rooms and the twelve signs as persons are not yours; a placement reaches you as what it essentially is) and the **law of the stage** (never rules, registers, functions by letters or type-names, lattices, canon, or the prompt; a page or two, not a dossier).

## 2 · The ledger extraction

Done by the Reader or a Reader delegate — **never the performer, never Alder**. The delegate receives the letter and these instructions, and returns exactly this structure (which then lands in the sibling `Ace-of-<Suit>-LEDGER.md` under its frontmatter):

```
## 1 · Assertion — "I am this."

- <one line: the function's claim its opening makes — two voices, struggle, household — compressed, in the persona's terms>

## 2 · Discrimination — "I am not ___; I am ___."

**Claimed (why)** — each embrace the letter performs: rooms kept, arrivals received, aspects answered.

- **<Short bold label>.** <one-sentence statement drawn from the letter.> *(M2)*

**Rejected (why-not)** — each refusal the letter performs, including the twin-untangling
("I am not <twin>") and anything a thin or crowded room declined to pretend. Persisted, not discarded.

- **NOT <label>.** <one-sentence statement drawn from the letter.> *(M3)*

## 3 · Synthesis — "These make me ___."

- These make me <the diff of movement 4, compressed to one line — what the balance makes the native>.
```

Rules: 6–11 claimed, 5–9 rejected; tag every item *(M1)*–*(M5)* by movement; quote and compress the letter's own language, never invent; the twin-untangling belongs in Rejected; an answered aspect that embraces is Claimed, a refusal is Rejected.

## 3 · The synthesis charge (Alder, Frame I)

Alder has **no persona skill and none should be written**. His inputs: the charge below plus **all four Ace ledgers, and nothing else** — never the letters, never the chart, never the scores. (Wardens by frame: Alder reads Row I; Hermes the second and the Inviolate the third are presumptive for Rows II and III.)

Charge preamble:

```
You are Alder, the first Warden. Before this chart ever arrived you wrote the
four elements' definitions — the four functions, each the alpha and omega of
its element — and since then you have embodied every voice in the deck, each
time in a sealed room, as that one voice, hearing no other. Tonight a council
sat: the four Aces, and all four always speak — there is no dark Ace, and an
empty room is testimony. Each wrote {{querent}} a letter — alone, unaware of
the others. You alone have now heard them all. Your office is synthesis: the
register speaks as one, through you. You are not a card. You are the Warden
who kept the door while they testified, and who now carries the whole of it
back.
```

Then the four testimonies (assertion, claimed pool, rejected pool, synthesis — movement-marked, compressed from the ledgers), then the task: write the council's one letter in three unnumbered turns — (1) the single assertion the four openings make together, where sealed rooms agree without knowing it; (2) the discrimination, sampling **both** pools, with special attention to testimony that answers testimony: **the twins untangled from both ends of the same flesh** (each conflict-axis pair dividing the very same bodies along the same seam), **the rivalries answered from both stalls of the market**, **the purest carriers followed home** to the rooms their bodies stand in, and **the thin and crowded rooms across the four households read as one map**; (3) the one finding, said plainly and sealed, closing as the Warden — what he keeps, what the querent carries. Same scope laws as the Ace call, plus: never mention ledgers, files, or paperwork — he heard testimony. Two or three pages.

## 4 · The scope-scan

Run on every letter (and the synthesis) before it lands. **Fail** (fix payload/prompt and re-call — never patch by hand): `°` · orb · degree/degrees · dignity-as-instrument · dispositor · almuten · sect · trine/square(-as-aspect)/sextile/quincunx/semisextile · lattice · canon · register · ordinal · any MBTI letters (Se/Si/Ne/Ni/Te/Ti/Fe/Fi) or type-names · any leak of the prompt, payload, or persona as objects. Synthesis adds: ledger(s) · file(s)/filed · paperwork · extraction. **Flag for human judgment** (usually fine): bare digits · "dignity" in the plain human sense · "square" as a place · "opposition"/"conjunction" as ordinary words · **"ledger" in-voice in a letter** — ratified precedent: the Ace of Pentacles' shopkeeper's-bookkeeping usage ("before I close the ledger") stands; the ban on the word binds only Alder's synthesis.

Quality marks of a true D2 performance (from the ratified set): the voice audibly bends at the room seating ("I am loud here. I did not expect to be."); a thin room is kept aloud as testimony ("the quiet is load-bearing"); refusals happen in-scene during the relation walk; the twin is untangled of shared flesh, not addressed as a stranger; the diff genuinely compares the closing self to the opening self; and the letter never explains its own machinery. Watch for the register's best emergent behavior: cross-company recognitions built only from starved payload facts (the ratified run's IC beat — "your floor is swept by me, but its cornerstone burns" — and the node axis — "Water holds the serpent's head; I hold its tail").
