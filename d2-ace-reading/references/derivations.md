## The four households (fixed, eternal)

## The population and the companies

**What counts (identical to D1):** planets, luminaries, the four angles (ASC, DSC, MC, IC), true and mean nodes, lunar apogees (True Lilith, Black Moon Lilith), Chiron, comets (e.g. Pholus), the four asteroids (Ceres, Pallas, Juno, Vesta), and the seven Hermetic lots. Each placement joins the company of its **occupied sign's element**; a company is the whole of the querent's placements in that element, and each Ace's rooms are that company sorted by sign.

**Fixed stars do not join companies in this register.** They enter only within a degree of a body — that contact adds +2 (either scoring option) — and are otherwise absent. Ruled precedent (2026-08-18): Vega–Sun at 1°07′ stands *just outside* the degree and is excluded; noted for the freeze reading rather than rounded in.

**Both node measures count** (true and mean), listed in a payload as one presence "twice measured" — same for the two Liliths, which are listed separately (the refusal found, and the refusal crowned).

## The three relations and the twin

Fixed per element; each Ace sees only its own three and none of the others':

- **Competitive** — the zodiacal opposite: **Fire↔Air, Earth↔Water.** Productive: not negative, not supportive; rivals over the same market whose treasuries can never hold each other's coin. The competitor closes movement 3 — the most productive encounter ends the walk.
- **Oblique ×2** — the remaining two elements: cautious, neither supporting nor inimical. One of them is the twin's company (met as a company, never as the twin). Order in performance: **non-twin oblique first, then the twin's company, competitor last.**
- **Twin** — the conflict-axis partner: **Fire↔Water, Earth↔Air.** Never an outward aspect; braided into the shared carriers. Movement 4 unbraids it: "I am not <twin>; I am <element>," said of shared flesh.

Axis roles, for the reader's bookkeeping: **conflict axes** (Fire/Water, Earth/Air) govern what bodies *carry*; **opposition axes** (Fire/Air, Water/Earth) govern what treasuries *admit*; the remaining pairs (Fire/Earth, Water/Air) are free routes — always ×1.

## Scoring — Option A (additive; each body funds the pole of its occupied sign)

Base 1 + type modifier + element modifier:

- **Planet** (Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn) +2; dominant match +2 / auxiliary match +1 / neither +0 → totals **5 / 4 / 3**
- **Luminary** (Sun, Moon) +2; in its own element +2 / neutral +0 / opposing zodiac element (fire↔air, water↔earth) −1 → totals **5 / 3 / 2**
- **Outer Lords** (Uranus=Air, Neptune=Water, Pluto=Fire) — single-element bodies, luminary template → **5 / 3 / 2**
- **Other bodies** (angles, nodes, Liliths, Chiron, comets, asteroids, lots) +1, no elemental modifiers → total **2**
- **Star contact** within a degree of a body: **+2** to that body's room.

Worked (ratified run): Saturn in Capricorn → 5 · Sun in Capricorn → 3 · Moon in Capricorn → 2 · Mars in Aries → 5 · Mercury in Aquarius → 5 (+2 Deneb) · Jupiter in Taurus → 3 (+2 Algol) · Pluto in Scorpio → 3.

## Scoring — Option B (decan streams into terrain treasuries)

One point per decan of the occupied sign (= 3) through **each elemental stream the body carries**, valued by the stream's relation to the terrain (the occupied sign's element): **share ×2 / neutral ×1 / opposing ×0** (opposition pairs fire↔air, water↔earth). Points **retain the body's elemental identity** (the currency) and are **deposited into the treasury of the occupied sign's element**, filed under that sign's center. Each treasury therefore holds at most 3 of 4 currencies — never its zodiacal opposite. Star contacts add +2 to the treasury, currency unassigned.

Worked (canon + ratified run): Sun in Capricorn → 3 Fire points into Earth's treasury, Heart · Mars in Aries → 6 Fire + 3 Water into Fire's treasury, Heart · Moon in Capricorn → 0 (Water inadmissible in Earth — intended) · Saturn in Capricorn → 6 Earth + 3 Air, Heart · Mercury in Aquarius → 6 Air + 3 Earth, Head.

**Open ruling, exercised 2026-08-18 and not yet ratified:** stream-less bodies — angles, nodes, Liliths, lots, asteroids, Chiron, Pholus — carry no currency under B and deposit 0. (Option A gives them 2 each; the divergence is the sharpest live specimen of the held tension — see the Gut-room finding in the worked example.)

**The A/B tension is held until freeze:** both computed, neither ranked, never conflated. A/B resolve *in use* downstream — A governs D3's inter-Page relations, B governs D3's intra-Page constitutions.

## Felt-volume banding (recorded, per the ratified run)

## Essential natures — the phrase-book

A placement reaches the persona as what it *is*. Canonical short forms (extend in kind; never add numbers, dignities, or aspect language):

**Bodies.** Sun — the core flame, identity's single light. Moon — keeper of tides and origins, memory, the one who tends what began. Mercury — the articulate mind, quick and exact. Venus — the lover of harmony, grace, the drawing-together. Mars — the will's blade: drive, heat, the fight carried in the body. Jupiter — the increaser: meaning, patronage, the open hand (if retrograde: walking backward, gifts turned inward). Saturn — the old authority: boundary, weight, time's discipline. Uranus — the breaker of patterns. Neptune — the dissolver: the ideal, the veil, the boundless behind forms. Pluto — the underworld power: what transforms by taking down to nothing and rebuilding.

**Points.** North Node — the soul's forward direction, appetite for the unlived (with the mean measure: the same head, twice measured). South Node — mastery already carried in, work finished before this life (the same tail, twice measured). True Lilith — the part that refuses structure. Black Moon Lilith — the refusal made sovereign, crowned in exile. Chiron — the wound that teaches. Pholus — the small cause with the vast release. Ceres — the great mother: harvest, grief, and the return from grief. Pallas — the strategist's wisdom, the pattern seen whole. Juno — the covenant of partnership, loyalty and its terms. Vesta — the tended flame, devotion kept burning in private.

**Angles.** Ascendant — the horizon of appearing, where the querent rises to meet the world. Descendant — the facing door, where the other arrives. IC — the floor of the chart: midnight, root, the private ground. MC — the crown of the meridian, the visible summit-point.

**Lots** (each named with its lord). Fortune, of the Moon — the body's own luck. Spirit, of the Sun — the soul's own initiative. Courage, of Mars — the fight the body is willing to have. Eros, of Venus — desire's true aim. Necessity, of Mercury — the unchosen obligation, what must be. Victory, of Jupiter — the promised triumph. Nemesis, of Saturn — the account that comes due.
