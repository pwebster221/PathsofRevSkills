# VenusFace distill — stage 2: PAINTERLY

Pull the card's vision into the deck's shared painterly medium via the hero
reference. Consumes `enrichment-context?track=painterly` (hero_asset_id —
409 if undesignated) + the card's approved vision content. Op: edit, ONE
reference image (the hero). Proven: Hierophant/Chariot/Lovers painterly
renders, 2026-07-08.

## Method

1. Open with the transfer instruction: "Create a tarot-card image in the
   EXACT painterly art style, brushwork, dramatic atmospheric lighting, and
   rich textural depth of the reference image — but an entirely different
   scene and palette."
2. Restate the card's scene (from its vision stage: the staged dragon, its
   realm, its pole directives — condensed, not re-derived).
3. **The palette rule, verbatim and non-negotiable:** "CRITICAL: keep this
   card's own colors — {card's decan palette} — do NOT use the reference's
   {hero palette}; borrow only its painterly technique, depth, and mythic
   weight." The hero transfers MEDIUM, never HUE (ADR-003). This clause is
   what kept the Lovers sage/rose against the Emperor's red — include it
   every time.
4. Close: portrait orientation, numeral, title.

## Guardrails

- One reference only: the deck hero. The card's own prior renders are not
  references at this stage.
- Expect medium-consistency, not mood-lock: each card keeps its own
  emotional weather (soft-luminous Lovers beside dark-volcanic Emperor is
  correct).
- The hero card itself skips this stage (its vision IS its painterly).
