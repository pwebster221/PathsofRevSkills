## Method

1. Open: "Merge these two images into a SINGLE integrated painterly scene
   of {CARD}."
2. Scope image 2's contribution exactly: "From image 2 (the gold symbolic
   sigil-plate) take ONLY the symbolic SHAPES, STRUCTURE and TEXTURE —
   {enumerate: the glyph-forms, the letter, the degree-marks, the decan
   sigils, the woven sigil-geometry}."
3. Bind the render to image 1: "Render those symbolic forms ENTIRELY in the
   painterly style, texture and {card palette} of image 1."
4. **The weave clause — the heart of the stage:** "The symbolic sigil-forms
   must be WOVEN INTO the painted scene as its living subjects and its very
   composition — the {dragon anatomy} ARE the {glyph}, the {letter/sigils}
   woven into the {scene elements}, the {card figure} itself the living
   sigil."
5. **The prohibitions, verbatim — these are what killed the rejected framed
   variant:** "Do NOT draw a gold border, frame, inset window or ornamental
   margin; do NOT keep the symbols apart from the scene; do NOT render them
   as flat gold linework."
6. Close: "One unified painterly image whose forms and structure ARE the
   {card} sigil itself, dark and luminous. Portrait orientation, title
   {CARD}."
