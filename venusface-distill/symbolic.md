# VenusFace distill — stage 3: SYMBOLIC

The card's glyphs as draconic sigil-work — the deck's shared symbolic
language. Consumes `enrichment-context?track=symbolic` (card attribution
from the persona §4 register + dragon_prompt's natal marks). Op: generate.
Proven: four sigil-plates in one uniform style, 2026-07-08.

## Method

Compose ONE symbolic device per card from its own correspondences, in the
shared grammar:

1. **Central device:** the card's astrological glyph FORMED FROM draconic
   anatomy (Aries from two dragon horns; Gemini from twin intertwined
   dragon-serpents; Cancer from two curled draconic claws) — the dragon IS
   the glyph, never beside it.
2. **The Hebrew letter** (persona §4 register: Heh, Vav, Zayin, Cheth…).
3. **Planet + degree** from the natal hook, exact (♂ 23°13′; ♃ 26°24′ ℞ —
   keep retrograde marks). Empty hooks render as what they are (the
   Chariot's 0°00′ empty bright ring).
4. **Three decan sigils** in the card's suit (wand/pentacle/sword/chalice
   sigils with their decan rulers).
5. **The numeral.**

Close every plate with the shared style string, verbatim: "rendered as
sacred draconic sigil-work: a symbolic composition of glyphs and letterforms
given a draconic cast, NOT a scene. Gold and pale-ash linework on a dark
parchment-and-shadow ground, ornamental and precise, illuminated-manuscript
symbolic style, draconic and heraldic. Symbols and letterforms only, no
landscape. Portrait orientation, a central symbolic device with the smaller
sigils arranged around it."

## Guardrails

- The shared style string is the deck's symbolic consistency — never vary it
  per card; only the symbols themselves change.
- No scene, no landscape, no narrative: this plate exists to be WOVEN into
  the painterly scene at stage 4, contributing shapes and texture only.
