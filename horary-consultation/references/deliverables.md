# Deliverables — Three Registers and a Worked Example

Each register is a translation of the one before, never a new reading. Findings may not appear in a later register that weren't established in an earlier one. Lead every register with the verdict.

## Register 1 — Technical judgment (for the operator)

Audience: the user running the consultation. Machinery fully visible. Structure:

1. **Cast block:** local time, UTC, JD, epoch, location, house system; compact placement list (planet, degree-sign, house; retrogrades flagged).
2. **Seating ruling** with its reason (relay rule, etc.).
3. **Significators** with turning arithmetic shown once.
4. **Considerations** noted; judged-anyway cautions stated.
5. **Testimonies:** dignity audit, receptions/dispositor loops, the quesited's condition (lord vs. house split — "circumstance reads 12th, essence reads exalted"), perfection mechanism with prohibition sweep, Moon's recent separations as prehistory.
6. **Timing skeleton** with the arithmetic shown once.
7. **Verdict, mechanism, cautions, outcome testimony.**
8. **Housekeeping:** chart_ids created, persistence/anonymity status, sunrise-verification results, Moon-uncertainty ranges.

Prose-forward; degree precision throughout; the user's terminology (e.g., Whole Sign default, epoch seconds) honored.

## Register 2 — Plain-words letter (for the person the question belongs to)

Voice: the *user's* voice to that person (first person), unless directed otherwise. Rules:

- **Keep the strongest images, translate the machinery.** "Peregrine — the wanderer, a planet with no place that receives it" survives; "L7 disposed by the Moon" becomes "she'll receive you, specifically." Degrees and house numbers vanish; "her planet," "the dog's planet," "the house of home" remain.
- The chart speaks ("the chart isn't judging her; it's describing her"); the writer relays.
- One verdict, two or three moves, the window stated in days and hours, the honest difficulty named ("it will be awkward and hard — go anyway").
- Dignity preserved for everyone described; one testimony of surviving care included when the chart shows it.
- The boundary paragraph is mandatory when the question touches another life: their road wasn't the question and isn't the reader's to carry.
- Resources, if included, are woven as plain sentences with at most two or three names and the essential numbers — never a list block.
- Close on relationship, not logistics (the offer to come along belongs at the end).

## Register 3 — Sequence-of-events report

A dated play-by-play document (markdown file unless another format is requested). Template:

```
# [Evocative title drawn from the reading's central image]
### Sequence of events, [date range]
*Drawn from the chart of your question — cast [datetime, place] — cross-checked against [natal sources].*

[Verdict paragraph: the answer up front; the fixed route; the fixed shape;
 the run-length; and the hard date named in bold.]

**[Weekday, date] — [beat name].**  [What perfects; what to do; one
 forecast detail if the chart gives one.]
  ... one block per beat, in strict chronological order, covering:
  the opening contact · THE WINDOW (with closing hour) · the first
  shift (window close) · the machinery engaging · the heavy personal
  days · the delivery deadline · THE HARD DATE (conduct rules +
  embedded crisis resources) · completion · the chapter turn (ingress
  cluster, completion-not-reopening framing)

**The standing rules** — each stated with its double confirmation
 (question-chart + natal layer): [through-them-not-around-them /
 channel rules / helper-not-rescuer].

**The ending, so it can be held on the hard days:** [the outcome
 testimony in plain words; the boundary sentence.]
```

Date-stamp discipline: every beat carries weekday + date; hours where the arithmetic supports them. Distinguish in the text **the catalyst** (the hard date — what destabilizes) from **the turn** (perfection + ingress cluster — what it precipitates).

## Real-world resource layer

When counsel includes concrete action, verify by web search *in the consultation, that day* — programs close, numbers change. Match resources to the chart's channels (a Mercury delivery-channel gets the appointment-based services; a 4th-house harbor gets the foster-during-treatment program; a confinement risk gets the legal co-entry path). Embed each at its timeline point in Register 3 and as plain sentences in Register 2. Two phone numbers maximum in the letter; the report may carry slightly more.

## Worked example (calibration) — "the ex's dog," June 2026

Question relayed by a friend: *how to help the ex's dog?* Ex homeless, in active addiction; dog a pit, bonded.

- **Cast:** June 11, 1:50 AM EDT, Manhattan, Whole Sign. Aries rising; Moon 26° Aries in the 1st; Neptune and Saturn also rising; Mars (L1) 17° Taurus in the 2nd; Venus (L7, the ex) 27° Cancer; Jupiter (6-from-7 = radical 12th Pisces → the dog) 26° Cancer, **exalted**, in the 4th.
- **Seating:** relay rule — the friend (querent Z.) took the Ascendant; the relaying user moved to the 11th (Saturn in Aries, in fall, disposed by L1: the friend frames the move but can't make it).
- **Radicality echoes:** the horary gave the dog to Jupiter — Z.'s natal Jupiter sits in his natal 6th (animals). The dog's horary sign (Pisces) holds Z.'s natal Moon *and* Saturn. Horary Mercury conjunct Z.'s natal Venus within 41'.
- **Decode:** Venus peregrine in Saturn's terms at the end of the sign of home = the wanderer about to lose even that; Neptune rising = the opiate field; the lord/house split = a well-loved dog in a terrible circumstance — so *the bond is the dog's strength; never counsel taking the dog.*
- **Mechanism:** Moon (Z.) translates light Jupiter→Venus by squares (the dog is the door; contact reaches her, with friction, inside a 48-hour reception window ending with Venus's ingress); Mercury translates Mars→Jupiter (the booked service delivers); Mars sextile Jupiter perfects at 29° (eleventh hour), all significators ingress within 72 hours after (chapter turn).
- **Synastry layer:** four-body Moon–Saturn stack at 8–13° Pisces — the dog's horary sign is the bond's center of mass; her Saturn glues his Sun/Moon/Venus; Venus–Mars score zero; no dignity exchanges (*binds without nourishing*); her SN on his Vesta at 0°04'; clean channel = Mercury (his words), live wire = her Mars opposite his Pluto at 0°15'.
- **Timeline:** window now→Sat sunrise; machinery June 17; devotion-and-release days June 18–20; delivery deadline June 22; **HARD DATE June 23** (transiting Mars on her natal Mars opposing both Plutos — nothing scheduled, nothing negotiated, prepared structures hold); completion June 27; turn June 28–30.
- **Deliverables produced:** technical judgment → letter in the user's voice (images kept: the wanderer, the door, the leash) → sequence report with the hard date — resources verified same-day (street-vet clinics; foster-during-treatment with phone; the ESA legal path that overrides breed rules) embedded at their timeline points.
