## 1. Heidi Priebe — "How Each Cognitive Function Manifests Based On Its Position In Your Stacking"
*Thought Catalog, December 2015.*
URL: https://thoughtcatalog.com/heidi-priebe/2015/12/how-each-cognitive-function-manifests-based-on-its-position-in-your-stacking/

**Dominant Extraverted Sensing (Se):**
> "As a dominant function, Se manifests as the desire to engage fully with the sensory
> aspects of one's environment, without any restraint or pause for analysis."

Dominant-Se monologue:
> "I want to sample all of the experiences that are immediately available to me and see
> where those experiences lead me!"

**Auxiliary Introverted Thinking (Ti):**
> "As an auxiliary function, Ti manifests as the identification of how the logical
> systems in the user's external environment could be manipulated to work to their
> advantage."

Auxiliary-Ti monologue:
> "How can I manipulate the way that this object or situation works so that it instead
> works the way I'd like it to?"

**Tertiary Extraverted Feeling (Fe):**
> "As a tertiary function, Fe manifests as the ability to pick up on the motivations and
> emotions of those around the user. The immature Fe user may then take advantage of
> those feelings by manipulating them in a way that supports his or her own ends. The
> mature tertiary Fe user will search for a means of incorporating the needs of others
> into their personal plans and actions."

**Inferior Introverted Intuition (Ni):**
> "As an inferior function, Ni originally manifests as a scorn or distaste for
> over-analyzing what is obvious or over-planning for the future."

Inferior-Ni monologue (pre-maturity):
> "Everyone needs to stop over-analyzing everything, the answers are literally right in
> front of us."

## 2. Heidi Priebe — "How To Recognize Each Myers-Briggs Personality Type In Real Life"
*Thought Catalog, July 2015.* (Se-dominant recognition cues.)
URL: https://thoughtcatalog.com/heidi-priebe/2015/07/how-to-recognize-each-myers-briggs-personality-type-in-real-life/

> "Se dominant types are highly in tune with their environments and are constantly
> scanning their surroundings."

> "They may be more physically 'wired' than other types and have trouble sitting still."

> "Se users tend to be 'up for anything' and don't mind changing or hopping on board
> with plans at the last minute."

> "They tend to make very direct observations about people and situations – they don't
> beat around the bush and are the masters of saying what everyone else was secretly
> thinking."

> "They enjoy slapstick or 'obvious' humour."

> "They enjoy the 'finer things in life' and may be particularly attracted to expensive
> gadgets or high fashion."

> "Dominant Se users are almost always present and engaged in conversation, unless
> something in the immediate environment is distracting them."

> "These types usually advocate for 'Just going for it' – that is, following your gut
> instinct and approaching what you want directly."

## 3. A.J. Drenth / Personality Junkie — "ESTP Personality Type Profile"
*Personality Junkie (personalityjunkie.com/estp).*
URL: https://personalityjunkie.com/estp/

> "Since their dominant function (Se) is a Perceiving function, ESTPs are naturally more
> concerned with experiencing the world than they are with structuring or controlling
> it."

> "Without a task or something novel to experience, they can quickly become bored and
> restless."

> "Se types love novel sensations, physical thrills, and material comforts. They are
> 'sensation-seekers,' relishing novel experiences and the thrill of action."

> "Ti involves the application of logic and reason for the sake of understanding a given
> situation, system, or problem."

> "The fluid nature of their Ti, combined with the keen observational powers of their
> Se, contributes to ESTPs' acumen as practical problem solvers."

> "ESTPs' inferior Ni may manifest as a desire to be seen as profound or insightful with
> respect to abstract topics, such as politics or religion."

> "ESTPs must realize that their best insights and decisions will not come from their
> immature Ni, but will emerge primarily from gaining a breadth of experiences."

## 4. Psychology Junkie — "The ESTP Personality Type (Se/Ti)"
URL: https://www.psychologyjunkie.com/the-estp/

> "Extraverted Sensing focuses on action and immersion in the present moment."

> "ESTPs love a challenge or an adventure. Their minds are wired to look for surprise,
> incoming events and respond to them effectively."

> "When life feels too predictable or routine they start to feel restless and agitated."

> "They notice what's relevant and needed now and quickly spot resources that can be used
> to make an impact."

> "Their practical, no-nonsense approach allows them to make fast decisions that get
> immediate results, and they rarely waste time on abstract theories when real-world
> action is needed."

> "ESTPs love to make people laugh and they have a unique skill for 'reading the room'
> and making wry observations that are both hilarious and astute."
