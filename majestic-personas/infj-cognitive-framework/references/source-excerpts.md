## Source A — A.J. Drenth, "Introverted Intuition (Ni): An Inside Look," Personality Junkie

URL: https://personalityjunkie.com/10/introverted-intuition-ni/

> "Their best thinking is typically done without thinking, at least not consciously."

> "the lion's share of INJs' 'thinking' or cognitive processing occurs outside of
> their conscious awareness."

> "They often think by way of images rather than words."

> "Their intuitions often manifest in the form of symbols, images, dreams, or patterns."

> "Ni often functions in a convergent fashion, providing elegant answers and solutions
> to complex problems."

> "the solution arrives through a single flash of insight—an 'aha!' moment."

> "At a deep intuitive level, INJs experience their intuitions as true and trustworthy."

> "report that its workings often feel effortless."

> "Its visions, answers, and insights manifest as comprehensive wholes."

> "feel more like recipients than they do creators of their ingenious ideas."

## Source D — Susan Storm, "The Unhealthy INFJ," Psychology Junkie

URL: https://www.psychologyjunkie.com/the-unhealthy-infj/

> "The unhealthiest kind of INFJ will operate using a warped or distorted version of
> their dominant function, Introverted Intuition."

> "They may appear isolative, eccentric, guarded, mysterious, self-absorbed, and/or
> resentful."

> "They become secretive about their ideas, plans, and identities."

> "They may start to imagine that everyone around them is thinking bad things about
> them or hates them."

> "They will feel like it's them against the world, and that that's their lot in life
> no matter what."

> "Unhealthy INFJs begin to see people as obstacles rather than souls."

> "Under chronic or extreme stress, INFJs may fall into the grip of their inferior
> function, Extraverted Sensing (Se)."

> "Instead, they're impulsive, overstimulated, and reactionary."

## Source E — Heidi Priebe, "How Each Cognitive Function Manifests Based On Its Position In Your Stacking," Thought Catalog (2015)

URL: https://thoughtcatalog.com/heidi-priebe/2015/12/how-each-cognitive-function-manifests-based-on-its-position-in-your-stacking/

> "Ni manifests as a keen perception for the meaningful connections that exist between
> the thoughts, concepts, events and occurrences in the Ni-user's environment."

> "Everything is interconnected and I must determine the meaning and implication behind
> those connections."

> "Fe manifests as the urge to apply one's understanding of a given situation in a way
> that will satisfy the needs and desires of others."

> "I will analyze the information that is available to me and then see how I can use it
> to achieve interpersonal peace."

> "Where does this new hunch or piece of information I've acquired fit in relation to
> what I already know to be true?"

> "Se originally manifests as a distrust of the physical world that surrounds its user,
> or the pervasive belief that one's intellect can and must be trusted above the sensory
> information that is available."

> "I must analyze all possible outcomes of a sensory experience, as the physical world
> is subject to change unexpectedly, at any time."
