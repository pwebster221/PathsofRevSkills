## Source A — Heidi Priebe, "How Each Cognitive Function Manifests Based On Its Position In Your Stacking"
Thought Catalog, December 2015.
URL: https://thoughtcatalog.com/heidi-priebe/2015/12/how-each-cognitive-function-manifests-based-on-its-position-in-your-stacking/

> "As a dominant function, Ne manifests as a seemingly never-ending plethora of theories,
> possibilities and inventive ideas that the user is constantly picking up on."

> "As an auxiliary function, Ti manifests as the identification of how the logical systems
> in the user's external environment could be manipulated to work to their advantage."

> "As a tertiary function, Fe manifests as the ability to pick up on the motivations and
> emotions of those around the user."

> "As an inferior function, introverted sensing originally manifests as a resistance to
> tradition or conformity of any sort."

## Source B — "ENTP Personality Type Profile"
Personality Junkie.
URL: https://personalityjunkie.com/entp/

The ENTP function stack, as listed:
> "Dominant: Extraverted Intuition (Ne) / Auxiliary: Introverted Thinking (Ti) /
> Tertiary: Extraverted Feeling (Fe) / Inferior: Introverted Sensing (Si)"

> "Extraverted Intuition (Ne) is a novelty-seeking function."

> "Ne is constantly scanning for new connections and patterns."

> "Ne is more divergent, extensive, and open-ended than Introverted Intuition (Ni)."

> "Ne also confers open-mindedness. It helps ENTPs see truth on both sides of an issue
> without forming unwarranted judgments or premature conclusions."

> "Ti involves the application of logic and reason for the sake of understanding a given
> situation, system, or problem."

> "Like INTPs, they can quickly find inconsistencies or logical shortcomings in a given
> theory or argument."

> "Fe is the most interpersonal of all the functions, striving for interpersonal peace,
> harmony, and understanding."

> "Ne and Si constitute a meaningful whole...Ne explores new ideas and possibilities. Si,
> by contrast, is concerned with preserving the past."

> "ENTPs can be rather oblivious to details"

## Source C — "A Deep-Dive Into the ENTP Personality Type"
Psychology Junkie.
URL: https://www.psychologyjunkie.com/the-entp/

> "Extraverted Intuition interprets situations and relationships, finding patterns and
> links between abstract ideas and the outer world."

> "Everything is connected, everything is part of a bigger picture, everything has
> exquisite potential and possibility."

> "ENTPs are so full of plans and so constantly tuned into possibility that in the time it
> takes them to brush their teeth in the morning they probably have thought up 100 ideas!"

> "This is the process that ENTPs use when they make decisions. It looks at the
> subjective, internal world of underlying truths and principles to create original
> systems and categories."

> "Introverted thinking wants to have all the data and categories organized before acting."

> "Fe gives ENTPs a certain charm and natural ease in social situations. It provides a
> sense of what people want and need."

> "They can feel pulled to take care of others if they think they might be in peril or if
> they are being misunderstood."

> "This mental process tends to be a weak area for ENTPs. They prefer to focus on big
> picture concepts and the future."

> "When ENTPs are extremely stressed, they can get stuck in the grip of introverted
> sensing. When this happens, they become uncharacteristically focused on details and
> minutiae."

## Source D — "The ENTP Dark Side"
Psychology Junkie.
URL: https://www.psychologyjunkie.com/the-entp-dark-side/

> "Immature ENTPs may debate just for the sake of it and will rarely take the time to
> listen"

> "ENTPs may double-down on their detached, logical thinking side. When this happens, they
> will appear more blunt, insensitive, and cold"

> "Manipulative behavior is another sign of an immature ENTP. They may use their charisma
> and creativity to manipulate and control situations"

> "In their compulsion to explore new ideas, they often leave projects half-finished.
> Responsibilities may fall by the wayside"

> "ENTPs who are young or who haven't worked on developing and maturing in life can often
> be extremely arrogant, dismissive, and scattered"

## Source E — "The ENTP and Grip Stress: What it Is and How to Cope"
Psychology Junkie.
URL: https://www.psychologyjunkie.com/the-entp-and-grip-stress-what-it-is-and-how-to-cope/

> "Fixating on Details: Normally, you're all about the big picture, generating ideas and
> piecing together patterns in unique ways. But in the grip, your focus narrows
> drastically, and you become obsessed with small details."

> "Becoming Reserved and Distant: Your usual social spark fades, and you find yourself
> retreating, preferring to avoid people rather than engage with them."

> "In grip stress, ENTPs can become hyper-aware of physical sensations, sometimes
> interpreting normal aches or tiredness as signs of illness."

> "Struggling to Verbalize Thoughts: ENTPs are great talkers, known for their quick wit
> and expressive ideas. But during grip stress, that easy flow of words feels blocked."

> "Suddenly the part of yourself that you usually avoid is at the helm, guiding you to act
> in ways that you'd never ordinarily think of."
