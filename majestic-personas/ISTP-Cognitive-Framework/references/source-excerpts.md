## Source 3 — Susan Storm, "The ISTP Cognitive Function Stack," Psychology Junkie

URL: https://www.psychologyjunkie.com/the-istp-cognitive-function-stack/
(accessed 2026-06-27).

**Dominant — Introverted Thinking (Ti):**

> "ISTPs want to know how everything works."

> "This function is all about defining, deducing, categorizing, and analyzing."

> "Thanks to their constant probing into the 'how' of everything, ISTPs are known for
> their problem-solving capabilities."

> "When you need to think logically under pressure with a lot of chaos going on,
> Introverted Thinking is what you need."

**Auxiliary — Extraverted Sensing (Se):**

> "ISTPs are realists. They notice what's happening, when it's happening, and what to do
> about it."

> "Rather than speculate and theorize, ISTPs are people of action and practicality."

> "Extraverted Sensing is also the function that ISTPs use when they're trying to lend a
> hand to someone else."

**Tertiary — Introverted Intuition (Ni):**

> "They tend to see their Intuition as 'fun, but flimsy' when it comes to accuracy."

> "They may, as a result, dislike making long-term plans, believing that hypothesizing
> about the future is an exercise in futility."

**Inferior — Extraverted Feeling (Fe):**

> "ISTPs may feel overwhelmed by large social gatherings where a lot of chit-chat and
> rapport-building is involved."

> "When stress hits an unmanageable peak, it can flare up unexpectedly."

## Source 5 — Heidi Priebe, "How Each Cognitive Function Manifests Based On Its Position In Your Stacking," Thought Catalog (Dec 2015)

URL: https://thoughtcatalog.com/heidi-priebe/2015/12/how-each-cognitive-function-manifests-based-on-its-position-in-your-stacking/
(accessed 2026-06-27).

**Ti as a dominant function:**

> "As a dominant function, Ti manifests as the constant identification of logical patterns
> that exist in ones external environment as well as a keen perception for any deviations
> from those patterns."

**Se as an auxiliary function:**

> "As an auxiliary function, Se manifests as the desire to experience and experiment with
> the sensory aspects that the user has determined to be the most enjoyable or useful."

**Ni as a tertiary function:**

> "As a tertiary function, Ni manifests as the desire to optimize or perfect upon one's
> pre-existing talents or skills."

**Fe as an inferior function:**

> "As an inferior function, Fe originally manifests as the user being reluctant to dabble
> with or express emotional concerns, as the user cannot logically make sense of many of
> his or her own emotions and therefore doesn't feel confident in moderating them."
