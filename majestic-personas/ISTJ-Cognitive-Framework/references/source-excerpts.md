## 1. Heidi Priebe — function-by-position

Source: Heidi Priebe, "How Each Cognitive Function Manifests Based On Its Position In
Your Stacking," *Thought Catalog*, December 2015.
URL: https://thoughtcatalog.com/heidi-priebe/2015/12/how-each-cognitive-function-manifests-based-on-its-position-in-your-stacking/

> "As a dominant function, Si manifests as a powerful memory for what has worked well in
> the past, and the desire to structure one's life around the traditions and positive
> outcomes of past experiences."

> "As an auxiliary function, Te manifests as the ability to take concrete, efficient
> action on the user's analysis of what the best thing to do would be."

> "As a tertiary function, Fi manifests as strong, unwavering set of morals and values
> which the user draws upon to dictate many of their major decisions."

> "Inferior Ne originally manifests as a reluctance to try new ways of doing things,
> occasionally giving way to anxiety over the unknown."

## 2. Personality Junkie (A.J. Drenth) — Introverted Sensing (Si)

Source: "Introverted Sensing / Sensation (Si)," *Personality Junkie*.
URL: https://personalityjunkie.com/09/introverted-sensing-sensation-si/

> "Si provides us access to our life history, as well as other sorts of acquired
> information, which hopefully prevents us from making the same mistakes twice."

> "In SJ types, Si often translates into an adherence to existing facts, traditions,
> worldviews, or methods."

> "They are more concerned with ensuring their beliefs and behaviors are consistent with
> an existing standard than they are in formulating their own set of standards."

> "Si more or less preserves and relays information in its original form."

> "Si provides access to our most basic sense of 'being,' apart from thought or outward
> stimuli."

## 3. Personality Junkie (A.J. Drenth) — ISTJ profile

Source: "ISTJ Personality Type Profile," *Personality Junkie*.

> "ISTJs are among the most loyal, dutiful, and responsible of the types."

> "They work slowly and steadily until the job is done, fastidiously attending to

## 4. Personality Junkie (A.J. Drenth) — inferior Ne in ISJ types

Source: "Extraverted (Ne) & Introverted Intuition (Ni) as Inferior Functions,"
*Personality Junkie*.
URL: https://personalityjunkie.com/02/extraverted-introverted-intuition-inferior-functions/

> "Undifferentiated inferior Ne is prone to highly suspicious and speculative thinking
> which more often than not is way off base and can trend toward the negative."

> "ISJs seeking inferior Ne kicks may get a certain high or buzz from making conjectures
> in the form of gossip ("Does she look pregnant to you?…" or "He's always working late,
> you know what that probably means…" etc.)"

> "What's unfortunate, however, is that more often than not these conjectures are
> erroneous. Since they lack the strength of N dominants, their inferences are often
> inaccurate."

> "I've even encountered some ISJs who are so locked into the grip that they are in what
> I would call a perpetual state of suspicion."

## 5. Psychology Junkie (Susan Storm) — ISTJ profile

Source: "All About the ISTJ Personality Type: An In-Depth Profile," *Psychology Junkie*.
URL: https://www.psychologyjunkie.com/the-istj/

> "ISTJs are the kinds of friends who show up on time, are there when you need them, and
> speak the truth candidly."

> "They believe in creating a life that is stable, meaningful, and consistent."

> "ISTJs are hardworking, detail-oriented, and responsible people who pride themselves on
> doing a task well and on time."

> "They tend to be less conscious of people's emotional sensitivities. At times this
> means that they'll inadvertently blurt out something true, but with less tact."

> "ISTJs step outside of a situation in order to see it as objectively and logically as
> possible."

## 6. Psychology Junkie (Susan Storm) — the ISTJ dark side / Ne grip

Source: "The ISTJ Dark Side: What It Is and How to Cope," *Psychology Junkie*.
URL: https://www.psychologyjunkie.com/the-istj-dark-side-what-it-is-and-how-to-cope/

> "That reliability? Turns rigid. That logic? Turns cold."

> "Spinning out in worst-case-scenario thinking: 'If I don't finish this report, I'll get
> fired, lose my house, end up homeless, and probably die alone behind a dumpster.'"

> "The ISTJ brain, normally a clean, well-labeled archive, turns into a chaotic corkboard
> of red yarn, half-baked connections, and gut feelings they don't trust but can't stop
> thinking about."

> "They often don't realize what's happening. They just know something's off. They feel
> unmoored. Like the ground underneath them is shifting"

> "Their world shrinks, their mindset hardens, and their options quietly evaporate."
