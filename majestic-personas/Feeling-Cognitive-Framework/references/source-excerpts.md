## THE OUTWARD VOICE — Extraverted Feeling (Fe)

### A.J. Drenth — "Extraverted Feeling (Fe): A Closer Look," *Personality Junkie*
URL: https://personalityjunkie.com/03/extraverted-feeling-fe/

> "Fe works broadly and extensively, while Introverted Feeling (Fi) penetrates more
> deeply and intensively. Fe is more concerned with collective morale, while Fi focuses
> more on the feelings of the self or a select few individuals."

> "When FJ types engage with others, they are looking to create a bond of shared
> feeling, especially "good" feeling. This requires they not only express their own
> feelings, but also perceive and interpret those of others. The hope is that feelings
> will be understood and reciprocated in a way that allows all parties to enjoy a sense of
> emotional resonance and harmony."

> "The satisfaction of creating rapport and emotional harmony also leads FJs to enjoy
> supporting and counseling others with emotional or relational difficulties."

> "It therefore seems important, from the FJ perspective, to get everyone on the same
> emotional wavelength."

> "In contrast to Te, Extraverted Feelers (i.e., FJ types), are more apt to wear their
> emotions on their sleeves; one rarely needs to guess about what FJs are feeling."

### Susan Storm — "The ESFJ's Cognitive Functions: An In-Depth Guide," *Psychology Junkie*
URL: https://www.psychologyjunkie.com/the-esfjs-cognitive-functions-an-in-depth-guide/

On dominant Extraverted Feeling (Fe):

> "the ability to read, absorb, and manage the emotions of a room like some kind of empathic wizard"

> "Fe is all about harmony, social responsibility, and emotional intimacy"

### Heidi Priebe — "How To Recognize Each Myers-Briggs Personality Type In Real Life," *Thought Catalog*
URL: https://thoughtcatalog.com/heidi-priebe/2015/07/how-to-recognize-each-myers-briggs-personality-type-in-real-life/

On dominant Fe:

> "Dominant Fe users talk almost exclusively about what other people are doing or thinking."

> "The moods of Fe users are particularly dependent on the moods of those around them – I.e. They will visibly and genuinely cheer up after hearing someone else's good news."

### Heidi Priebe — "How Each Cognitive Function Manifests Based On Its Position In Your Stacking," *Thought Catalog*
URL: https://thoughtcatalog.com/heidi-priebe/2015/12/how-each-cognitive-function-manifests-based-on-its-position-in-your-stacking/

Dominant Extraverted Feeling (Fe):

> "As a dominant function, Fe seeks to identify what is moral by identifying what those
> around them value, and then to enforce those values as a measure of keeping the peace
> in their external environment."

## THE INWARD VOICE — Introverted Feeling (Fi)

### A.J. Drenth — "Introverted Feeling (Fi) vs. Ti, Ni, & Fe," *Personality Junkie*
URL: https://personalityjunkie.com/12/introverted-feeling-fi-vs-ti-ni-fe/

> "Like the other introverted functions, Fi is characteristically intensive rather than extensive."

> "More specifically, it is focused on navigating and managing the FP's personal feelings, tastes, and values."

> "Rather than distributing its feelings and energies across a breadth of individuals (as Fe does), Fi concentrates its gaze on the self or the 'subject.'"

> "Introverted Feeling also works to shape its own worldview—a personalized system of values—that can serve as a platform for self-understanding and decision-making."

> "There is little IFPs deplore more than those who seem fake, showy, or disingenuous."

### A.J. Drenth — "The 8 Functions: Roles, Images & Characteristics," *Personality Junkie*
URL: https://personalityjunkie.com/functions-ni-ti-fi-si-ne-te-fe-se/

On Introverted Feeling (Fi), "Valuing":

> "FPs also use their personal values as a compass for navigating life."

### Susan Storm — "The ISFP's Cognitive Functions: A Guide," *Psychology Junkie*
URL: https://www.psychologyjunkie.com/the-isfps-cognitive-functions-a-guide/

On dominant Introverted Feeling (Fi):

> "Introverted Feeling focuses on what is deeply important to the self."

### Heidi Priebe — *The Comprehensive INFP Survival Guide*, Thought Catalog Books, 2016
ISBN 978-1-945796-15-9. On dominant Introverted Feeling (Fi):

> "Introverted feeling is both a highly emotional and a highly analytical function. It
> intuitively picks up on emotional nuances in its environment and internally deciphers
> those nuances—seeking to uncover the deeper meaning or emotional truth behind any given
> situation."

> "Above all else, introverted feeling seeks truth and authenticity. Because Fi serves
> as its own moral compass—evaluating situations through an internal, intuitive analysis
> of right and wrong—it does not mind going against the grain when it comes to what it
> knows to be right. Fi users must be true to themselves and what they believe in above
> absolutely everything else."

> "Introverted feeling is a river. It's the steady ebb and flow of emotion, of
> understanding, of deep, resounding truths that curve and flow their way through the
> INFP's consciousness…"

### "FiSe (ISFP)," *Type in Mind*
URL: https://www.typeinmind.com/fise

> "Fi assigns meaning to everything."

## THE SHADOW — the grip pole of each attitude

### Fe in the grip of inferior Introverted Thinking (Ti)
Susan Storm — "What You're Like During Grip Stress, Based On Your Myers-Briggs Personality
Type," *Psychology Junkie* (ENFJ/ESFJ section; grip-stress framework from Naomi Quenk).
URL: https://www.psychologyjunkie.com/what-youre-like-during-grip-stress-based-on-your-myers-briggs-personality-type/

> "When you are in the grip of your inferior function, you become uncharacteristically
> withdrawn and more detached from the world around you. Other people's feelings feel
> invasive rather than welcome and you experience a numb sensation emotionally."

### Fi in the grip of inferior Extraverted Thinking (Te)
Susan Storm — "The ISFP Personality Type," *Psychology Junkie*.
URL: https://www.psychologyjunkie.com/the-isfp/

> "Under stress, ISFPs may overuse feeling and sensing and as a result, fall into the
> grip of their inferior thinking function."

> "They can become uncharacteristically critical, focused on organizing and fixing
> problems, and righting wrongs."
