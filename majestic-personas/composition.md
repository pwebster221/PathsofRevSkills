## What this document is — and is not

**This is a derivation map, not an authoring instruction.** The Majestic tier's persona
instruments are grounded in the **cognitive-function stack** (the MBTI generator: suit =
dominant function, attitude + auxiliary-element relation = rank), and that is their
scoring identity. The decan holdings recorded here are the *source data the type
assignments were derived from* — they *do not enter the Majestic voices* and must not
be used to re-author, re-derive, or "correct" the 20 skills in this directory. A court
scores as a perspective (four functions in one ordered stack), not as a decan-word.
That is a canon decision, not an omission: forcing the decan-word treatment onto the
courts would collapse them into shifted zodiac Majors and surrender the one thing this
tier uniquely contributes — a grid of perspective combinations not found in the stars.

What this map preserves is the **bridge**: the courts are *also* viewable as decan
triads, and that dual viewability is the deck's passage from Minor to Major. Keep the
bridge documented; walk the voices on the MBTI side.

## The passage up the tarot

The Majestic tier is the **transitional texture** between the pure letters (Minors) and
the generative words (Majors). The language climbs in four steps:

1. **Aces — the elemental culmination.** Four pure cognitive-function pairs (Ni+Ne,
   Fi+Fe, Ti+Te, Si+Se), the function itself above any placement. No decans; pure
   element.
2. **Pages — the seasonal thrones.** Each anchors its element *as a whole season*
   (seated at the cross-quarter festival at the season's height), not a sign. No decan
   triad; the element in time.
3. **Knights, Queens, Kings — the shifted triads.** The 36 decans are rearranged and
   divided among the twelve: each court spans **20°–20° across two signs** — the *last
   decan of the prior sign* plus the *first two decans of its dominant sign*. Rank
   carries modality: **Queen = cardinal, Knight = fixed, King = mutable.** The result
   mirrors the zodiac, but **off by one**.
4. **The zodiac Majors — the correction.** The twelve signs re-cover the same 36 as
   center-pure, single-element triads. The shift is resolved; the letters align; the
   generative cycle of the Majors begins.

## The off-by-one law (the shift the zodiac corrects)

The shift is not approximate — it is exactly **+1 on the Enneagram number line**, and it
is visible in the triads:

- **Courts run the raw number line:** every Queen holds types **{1, 2, 3}**, every
  Knight **{4, 5, 6}**, every King **{7, 8, 9}** — strict numeric triads, each crossing
  a center boundary (one type imported from the previous center + two native to the
  rank's own).
- **The zodiac Majors run the centers:** cardinal signs hold **{2, 3, 4}** (Heart),
  fixed **{5, 6, 7}** (Head), mutable **{8, 9, 1}** (Body) — center-pure triads.
- **The correction is +1.** {1,2,3}→{2,3,4}, {4,5,6}→{5,6,7}, {7,8,9}→{8,9,1}. The
  courts are the not-yet-aligned pass through the 36; the zodiac field shifts the frame
  by one and the triads land on the centers.

Two structural corollaries of the shift:

- **The visitor decan is always the triad's leading type** (Queens import a 10/type-1,
  Knights a 4, Kings a 7) — each court's numeric triad *begins* with the imported
  letter and completes with its own suit.
- **The visitor suit is fixed per suit**, following the zodiac wheel's element order
  (the sign before fire is water, before water air, before air earth, before earth
  fire): Wands courts always carry a **Chalices** tail, Chalices carry **Swords**,
  Swords carry **Pentacles**, Pentacles carry **Wands**. This is the "rearrangement of
  texture": every court is two-thirds pure element + one-third the tail of the
  preceding element.

## The remapping law (personal function→element splits)

The canonical split (Wands/Fire = Intuition, Chalices/Water = Feeling, Swords/Air =
Thinking, Pentacles/Earth = Sensing) is one of exactly **8 valid assignments out of the
24 possible** — verified by full enumeration (2026-07-08). A practitioner may re-split
the four functions across the four elements to match how they carry their own stack,
and the generator will still produce all 16 types, **if and only if** the split obeys
one rule:

> **The two perceiving functions (N, S) must occupy one silent diagonal — {Fire, Earth}
> or {Air, Water}, the element pairs with no relation — and the two judging functions
> (T, F) the other.**

Why: MBTI dominant–auxiliary is always one perceiver + one judger — never N–S, never
T–F. The element grid's two relations (opposing: Fire↔Water, Air↔Earth; complementary:
Fire↔Air, Water↔Earth) exist to host dom–aux pairings, so the two same-kind pairs,
which can never meet as dom–aux, must be exiled to the two relationless diagonals. Then
every opposing and complementary edge automatically carries a legal perceiver+judger
pairing.

- **Count:** 2 (which diagonal takes perception) × 2 (N/S order) × 2 (T/F order) = 8.
  These are exactly the symmetries of the elemental square — the canonical assignment
  plus its rotations and reflections.
- **What breaks otherwise:** in each of the other 16 splits, all 8 opposing-rank courts
  (every Page and Knight) generate perceiver–perceiver or judger–judger stacks — types
  that do not exist. Only the Queens and Kings survive, yielding 8 types; the two
  dom–aux families exiled to the diagonals become unreachable. A deck with no youth
  ranks.
- **Worked examples:** N=Air with T=Fire is valid *only* with the forced completion
  S=Water, F=Earth (each function's partner takes its diagonal) — all 16 generate, with
  the court families reshuffled. But S=Fire, N=Water, F=Air, T=Earth puts {N,S} on an
  opposing pair and {T,F} on the other: only the NT/SF Queens and Kings survive, and
  the entire NF and ST families are unreachable.
- **Invariance:** under any of the 8 valid splits, the 20 persona skills in this
  directory **do not change** — the corpus is invariant. Only the card→type routing
  table moves: a remapping re-labels which card summons which type-skill; the
  perspectives themselves stand.
