## A.J. Drenth — "Extraverted Thinking (Te): A Closer Look" (Personality Junkie)

URL: https://personalityjunkie.com/10/extraverted-thinking-te/

The OUTWARD voice (Te — source / alpha). Te serves as the dominant or auxiliary function for
TJ types (INTJ, ENTJ, ISTJ, ESTJ).

> "It is characteristically impersonal, focused more on things and systems than people or
> feelings."

> "TJs readily express their rational judgments; they literally think (i.e., make logical
> judgments, conclusions, and decisions) aloud."

> "Their direct, 'to the point' style is sometimes perceived by others as harsh, blunt, or
> tactless."

> "Te strives to make the external world and its operations more rational, employing precise
> definitions, policies, plans, and procedures."

> "Te approaches and structures things in explicitly rational ways."

> "TJs are known to pay close attention to the way things are ordered, ensuring that they
> comport with the appropriate linear sequence or hierarchical structure."

> "Quantification is another hallmark feature of the Te approach."

> "Te, as we've seen, also aligns itself with the scientific method or with objective methods
> in general."
>
> (Used in `SKILL.md` as the trailing contiguous substring: "aligns itself with the scientific
> method or with objective methods in general.")

> "Te, by contrast, tends to be more confident and forward-moving (especially in ETJs), quick
> to make changes and reforms in response to the latest data."

> "The extraverted functions are constantly adding and broadening, while the introverted
> functions tend to reduce and deepen."

This page also contrasts the two attitudes of the Thinking function:

> "Introverted Thinking (Ti) is a more implicit and subjective form of logic."

> "Rather than looking outward and referencing objective standards, it reasons and operates
> according to its own inner criteria."
>
> (In `SKILL.md` this is embedded mid-sentence as "[r]ather …", with the bracket flagging the

## A.J. Drenth — "Introverted Thinking (Ti): An Inside Look" (Personality Junkie)

URL: https://personalityjunkie.com/04/introverted-thinking-ti-intp-vs-intj-extraverted-thinking/

The INWARD voice (Ti — end / omega). Ti serves as the dominant function for INTP and ISTP,
and the auxiliary function for ENTP and ESTP.

> "As an introverted function, Ti relies on its own inner logic and manufactures its own ways
> of doing things."

> "Instead of referencing external protocols and 'doing things by the book' (Te), Ti is
> confident in its ability to spontaneously and independently figure things out."

> "As an introverted judging function, Ti prefers to make its own independent assessments and
> utilize its own evaluative criteria, the validity of which are experienced as self-evident."

> "It is relatively unconcerned with whether others agree with its methods or conclusions."

> "Ti is more reductive than Te. It attempts to strip away particulars and boil down
> pluralities so that only what is essential or foundational remains."

> "Ti trusts and embraces its own subjectivity, considering it a useful and necessary tool for
> discerning truth, and especially wisdom."

> "If something is perceived as irrelevant to its current objectives, Ti will ignore or
> dismiss it."

> "The independent and self-referencing nature of Ti has largely contributed to ITPs'
> reputation as armchair philosophers."

C.G. Jung on the introverted thinker, quoted by Drenth on this page:

> "With the intensification of his type, his convictions become all the more rigid and
> unbending."

## Heidi Priebe — "How Each Cognitive Function Manifests Based On Its Position In Your Stacking" (Thought Catalog, December 2015)

URL: https://thoughtcatalog.com/heidi-priebe/2015/12/how-each-cognitive-function-manifests-based-on-its-position-in-your-stacking/

The Thinking function as a **dominant** function, in each attitude.

**Extraverted Thinking (Te) as a dominant function** (the King / Knight of Swords root):
> "As a dominant function, Te manifests as the ability to clearly envision the most effective
> outcome to any given situation or problem and the ability to set the corresponding plans
> into action."

**Introverted Thinking (Ti) as a dominant function** (the Queen / Page of Swords root):
> "As a dominant function, Ti manifests as the constant identification of logical patterns
> that exist in ones external environment as well as a keen perception for any deviations from
> those patterns."

(Note: "ones" appears without an apostrophe in the source and is retained verbatim.)
