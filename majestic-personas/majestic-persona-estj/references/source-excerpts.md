# Source Excerpts — ESTJ (Te–Si–Ne–Fi)

Verbatim, attributed passages used as the authoritative source for the
`ESTJ-Cognitive-Framework` persona (the Knight of Swords interior). Every quotation
that appears in `SKILL.md` is reproduced here word-for-word with its citation. Anything
in `SKILL.md` that is NOT enclosed in quotation marks is the persona author's own
framing or a summary, not a quotation.

Sources are secondary cognitive-function literature on the ESTJ (there is no single
Priebe "ESTJ Survival Guide" volume parallel to the INFP guide; the closest Priebe
primary material is her cognitive-function writing, supplemented by the standard
Personality Junkie / Psychology Junkie / Type in Mind treatments of the Te–Si–Ne–Fi
stack and its grip/stress pole).

## 1. Heidi Priebe — "How To Recognize Each Myers-Briggs Personality Type In Real Life"

Thought Catalog, July 2015.
<https://thoughtcatalog.com/heidi-priebe/2015/07/how-to-recognize-each-myers-briggs-personality-type-in-real-life/>

On Extroverted Thinking (Te) dominant types (ESTJ / ENTJ):

> "Extroverted thinking types are extremely goal oriented. They will usually ask you
> about your own goals, either subtly or overtly."

> "They enjoy talking about plans they have and the expected outcomes of those plans."

> "Te users are bothered by inefficiency and strongly dislike people being late or
> veering off topic if there's work to be done."

> "They will always argue from a results-based, purely practical standpoint."

## 2. Heidi Priebe — "How Each Cognitive Function Manifests Based On Its Position In Your Stacking"

Thought Catalog, December 2015.
<https://thoughtcatalog.com/heidi-priebe/2015/12/how-each-cognitive-function-manifests-based-on-its-position-in-your-stacking/>

> "As a dominant function, Te manifests as the ability to clearly envision the most
> effective outcome to any given situation or problem and the ability to set the
> corresponding plans into action."

> "As an auxiliary function, Si manifests as the preference to rely on the tried-and-true
> method when working to accomplish a particular goal."

> "As a tertiary function, Ne manifests as a series of creative solutions that may go
> into solving a given problem or moving them towards a goal."

> "As an inferior function, Fi manifests as a general disdain for emotional expression
> and a fear of being perceived as 'weak' by others."

## 3. A.J. Drenth — "ESTJ Personality Type Profile"

Personality Junkie. <https://personalityjunkie.com/estj-type-profile/>

> "Te strives to bring order, control, and rationality to the systems and operations of
> the outside world."

> "ESTJs Judge before they Perceive, speak before they listen."

> "Te is oriented toward quantification, insisting on objective standards and measurable
> goals."

> "Like other SJ types, ESTJs function as conservators of the past."

> "ESTJs are dutiful, hardworking, and task-oriented."

> "Their Si prefers a more routine and predictable lifestyle."

> "Because their inner Judging function (Fi) is inferior, they may feel they have
> relatively little inner control."

> "Finding inner control elusive, they naturally turn their focus outwardly, hoping that
> achieving outer control will bring them inner calm and security."

## 4. Psychology Junkie — "The ESTJ Personality Type (Te/Si)"

<https://www.psychologyjunkie.com/the-estj/>

On the function stack:

> "ESTJs lead with Extraverted Thinking, a cognitive function that focuses on external,
> objective information and logic to make decisions."

> "They know how to step into any situation and bring a sense of order and structure to
> it; going after objectives in the most pragmatic way possible."

> "The second function for the ESTJ, Introverted Sensing, is concerned with recalling
> facts, details, and past experiences."

> "They value tradition and routine, often sticking with what has proven to work in the
> past."

> "The tertiary function, Extraverted Intuition, is less pronounced but still impactful."

> "This function often shows up in the ESTJ's sense of humor."

> "Finally, ESTJs' least conscious function, Introverted Feeling, is in the inferior
> position."

> "It's centered on internal values and feelings, which ESTJs may struggle to understand
> or express."

On strengths:

> "ESTJs are incredibly reliable and are often seen as the 'rock' in their relationships."

> "ESTJs are natural-born leaders who are comfortable in positions of authority."

> "With Extraverted Thinking as their dominant function, ESTJs are highly organized,
> preferring structure and order."

> "If you give an ESTJ a task, chances are they'll have it done ahead of schedule."

> "ESTJs are clear and direct in their communication. They value honesty and
> straightforwardness."

> "Loyalty is a significant trait for ESTJs, they stand by those they care about and
> their commitments."

> "ESTJs believe in responsibility, following through on their commitments, being moral
> citizens, and being competent in their careers."

## 5. Psychology Junkie — "The Unhealthy ESTJ"

<https://www.psychologyjunkie.com/the-unhealthy-estj/>

Describing the immature / unhealthy ESTJ and the inferior-Fi grip:

> "dominating, aggressive, controlling, and confrontational"

> "Their way is the only 'right' way, and they're quick to shoot down other opinions"

> "may be dismissive of other people's feelings and adopt a 'just get over it' attitude"

> "see it as overly-sensitive, useless, time-wasting, weak, or self-absorbed"

> "see things in extremely black and white contexts, jump to conclusions quickly"

> "stubbornly refuse relying on others or any new or progressive ways of doing things"

> "change is anathema to them"

> "increasingly withdrawn, emotional, hyper-sensitive, and fall prey to a 'martyr' complex"

> "misinterpret small, insignificant details into personal attacks and feel that everyone
> is against them"

> "resist seeing how their actions go against their own values and what they say they
> believe in"

## 6. Type in Mind — "TeSi (ESTJ)"

<https://www.typeinmind.com/tesi>

On the felt interior of the TeSi (ESTJ) mind:

> "Te is all about effectiveness and getting things done."

> "They are primarily concerned with moving forward and are not interested in wasting
> time."

> "The most effective functional or workable solution is often obvious to them."

> "They are sure of themselves, their views, and their morals."

> "They see the external world as a world of possibilities that can change at any moment,
> while they are internally very solid."

> "Fi is the small part of the TeSi that is quite vulnerable and sensitive."

> "Their emotions, while perhaps uncomplicated, are felt very deep down."

> "Talking about their emotions may be quite difficult for the TeSi."
