## Source A — Susan Storm, "The ISFP's Cognitive Functions: A Guide," *Psychology Junkie*
URL: https://www.psychologyjunkie.com/the-isfps-cognitive-functions-a-guide/

### Dominant — Introverted Feeling (Fi)
> "Introverted Feeling focuses on what is deeply important to the self."

> "living in harmony with deeply-held core values is of utmost importance."

> "they focus on intangible values—the ones that truly matter when life nears its end."

### Auxiliary — Extraverted Sensing (Se)
> "Extraverted Sensing, the auxiliary function for ISFPs, is all about living in the present moment."

> "ISFPs, through Extraverted Sensing, have a deep appreciation for the beauty and richness of the world."

> "Se provides a direct pipeline to the physical world, enabling ISFPs to immerse themselves."

### Tertiary — Introverted Intuition (Ni)
> "ISFPs utilize Ni in a playful, recreational context, providing a spark to their creativity."

### Inferior — Extraverted Thinking (Te)
> "Te is not the ISFP's natural realm."

## Source B — A.J. Drenth, "ISFP Personality Type Profile," *Personality Junkie*
URL: https://personalityjunkie.com/isfp-type-profile/

> "ISFPs' dominant function is Introverted Feeling (Fi)."

> "Fi is a Judging function, it is characteristically more serious than it is light and playful."

> "Extraverted Sensing (Se) serves as ISFPs' auxiliary function."

> "ISFPs use Introverted Intuition (Ni) as their tertiary function, which may lead them to develop some degree of interest in abstract or theoretical topics."

> "ISFPs must work to understand the ways in which their inferior function, Extraverted Thinking (Te), manifests in their personality."

> "ISFPs form deep emotional attachments to their loved ones, contributing to a strong sense of loyalty and devotion."

> "ISFPs often display a high concern for, as well as good taste in, fashion and aesthetics."

> "Se types can often be found scanning the environment for interesting sensory novelties."

## Source C — Susan Storm, "The ISFP Fi-Ni Loop: When Imagination Outruns Reality," *Psychology Junkie*
URL: https://www.psychologyjunkie.com/the-isfp-fi-ni-loop-when-imagination-outruns-reality/

> "A 'loop' happens when you get stuck cycling between your dominant and tertiary functions, while leaving out your auxiliary function."

> "Fi and Ni are both introverted functions, so they feel safe and familiar. They let you retreat into your internal world, but the problem is, the more time you spend there, the further you drift from reality."

> "When ISFPs get stuck in this loop, they often: Complain about their situation but fail to take action. Become fatalistic, only imagining negative outcomes for the future. Withdraw from the world, becoming disengaged from reality."

> "Without Se to ground you, you start to feel overwhelmed, stuck, and out of touch with the world around you."

## Source D — Susan Storm, "The ISFP Personality Type," *Psychology Junkie*
URL: https://www.psychologyjunkie.com/the-isfp/

> "Like all Adventurers, ISFPs live in the moment and enjoy taking advantage of opportunities as they arise."

> "As sensing types, ISFPs have a keen eye for beauty and details in their outer world."

> "They tend to keep their feelings tucked inside and share them with only a trusted few."

> "Under stress, ISFPs may overuse feeling and sensing and as a result, fall into the grip of their inferior thinking function."

> "They can become uncharacteristically critical, focused on organizing and fixing problems, and righting wrongs."

## Source E — "FiSe (ISFP)," *Type in Mind*
URL: https://www.typeinmind.com/fise

> "Fi is a decision making-process that is very interested in determining its own moral code and what the FiSe's gut instinct tells them is right"

## Source F — Susan Storm, "The Unhealthy ISFP," *Psychology Junkie*
URL: https://www.psychologyjunkie.com/the-unhealthy-isfp/

### Healthy ISFP
> "adaptable yet sensitive individuals who value authenticity and integrity above all else"

### Unhealthy / immature ISFP (the same drives turned against themselves)
> "unhealthy ISFPs take things too personally and can become easily offended"

> "especially vulnerable to criticism, whether real or perceived"

> "They may overestimate or idealize their emotions and feelings, believing that their gut instincts are always right"

> "they will reach a point where they stop listening to truth or accepting more facts or data because they are sticking so closely to a conviction or value"

> "excessively rigid and inflexible, finding it difficult to consider new ways of doing things"

> "label people who disagree with them or cause them hardship as inherently evil and blame them for their problems"

> "get stuck in past hurts and carry grudges for extended periods of time"

> "easily overwhelmed or procrastinating, and they may find it difficult to stay on task"

> "become so detached from people that 'hermit' seems like an understatement"
