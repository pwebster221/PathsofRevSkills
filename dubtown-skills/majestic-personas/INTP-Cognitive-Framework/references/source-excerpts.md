# Source Excerpts — INTP (Ti-Ne-Si-Fe)

Verbatim, attributed passages from the web sources fetched while authoring the
`INTP-Cognitive-Framework` persona for the Queen of Swords. Each quotation below was
retrieved from the cited URL (fetched 2026-06-27). Passages are retained in the original
authors' words. Where a source did not yield a verbatim line for a given idea, the SKILL.md

## A. Heidi Priebe — "How Each Cognitive Function Manifests Based On Its Position In Your Stacking" (Thought Catalog)

URL: https://thoughtcatalog.com/heidi-priebe/2015/12/how-each-cognitive-function-manifests-based-on-its-position-in-your-stacking/

> "As a dominant function, Ti manifests as the constant identification of logical patterns
> that exist in ones external environment as well as a keen perception for any deviations
> from those patterns."

> "Auxiliary Ne manifests as a plethora of possibilities that arise to support or expand
> upon a thought or decision that the user has come to."

> "As a tertiary function, introverted sensing manifests as a proneness to nostalgia, as
> well as a method of contrasting the new and exciting with the old and the known."

> "As an inferior function, Fe originally manifests as the user being reluctant to dabble
> with or express emotional concerns, as the user cannot logically make sense of many of his
> or her own emotions and therefore doesn't feel confident in moderating them."

*This article gives the canonical INTP stack: Dominant Ti, Auxiliary Ne, Tertiary Si,
Inferior Fe.*

## B. Heidi Priebe — "18 Telling Differences Between ENTP And INTP Personality Types" (Thought Catalog)

URL: https://thoughtcatalog.com/heidi-priebe/2016/05/18-telling-differences-between-entp-and-intp-personality-types/

> "INTPs lead with Introverted Thinking – a function that seeks to verify, rationalize and
> understand its environment without bias."

> "INTPs analyze first and brainstorm second."

> "INTPs are highly invested in developing an unbiased perception of themselves."

> "INTPs prefer to be completely alone when they're alone, so they can ensure their thought
> process won't be interrupted."

> "INTPs are often skeptical of personality theory and will only begin to trust it once they
> consistently test as an INTP."

> "INTPs are on an eternal quest for truth, backed up by possibility."

## C. Personality Junkie (A.J. Drenth) — "Introverted Thinking (Ti): An Inside Look (INTP vs. INTJ)"

URL: https://personalityjunkie.com/04/introverted-thinking-ti-intp-vs-intj-extraverted-thinking/

> "As an introverted function, Ti relies on its own inner logic and manufactures its own
> ways of doing things."

> "Insistent on marching to the beat of their own drummer, INTPs and ISTPs are the most
> fiercely independent of all types."

> "It is largely Ti that compels NTPs to understand the essence of things, including their
> essential self, purpose, and philosophy."

> "Ti prefers to make its own independent assessments and utilize its own evaluative
> criteria, the validity of which are experienced as self-evident."

> "Ti trusts and embraces its own subjectivity, considering it a useful and necessary tool
> for discerning truth, and especially wisdom."

> "Ti...serves as a filter for incoming information. If something is perceived as irrelevant
> to its current objectives, Ti will ignore or dismiss it."

## D. Personality Junkie (A.J. Drenth) — "Introverted Thinking (Ti) & Extraverted Feeling (Fe) in TP Types"

URL: https://personalityjunkie.com/02/introverted-thinking-ti-extraverted-feeling-fe-intps-entps-istps-estps/

> "For TP types, Ti is the more conscious member of the function pair."

> "TPs tend to see themselves as highly autonomous agents. They enjoy bucking Te rules and
> protocols in favor of doing things in their own way and on their own time."

> "Most TPs tend to wither without some degree of social contact. If isolated for too long,
> or if their work seems devoid of a socially meaningful objective, they can start feeling
> depressed or listless."

> "Fe connections often serve as comforting 'background noise' for TP types."

> "Ti and Fe exist in a constant state of tension with one another. This is especially
> palpable for ITPs, for whom these functions serve as the dominant and inferior bookends of
> their function stack."

> "TPs often envision some form of idealized Fe as their explicit life purpose."

## E. Personality Junkie (A.J. Drenth) — "The Inferior Function in Action: Real-Life Examples"

URL: https://personalityjunkie.com/01/inferior-function-action-real-life-examples/

## F. Psychology Junkie — "The Unhealthy INTP"

URL: https://www.psychologyjunkie.com/the-unhealthy-intp/

> "They can assume that they are right all the time and just shut people out who question

> "They may repress their own emotions, assuming they are irrelevant"

> "They become increasingly reclusive and lonely and set in their ways"

> "INTPs who repress Ne can become very rigid in their stance on things"

*This article supplies the healthy→unhealthy (gift→wound) contrast used in §3 of the
persona: the same INTP strengths curdling into arrogance, brutality, emotional repression,
reclusiveness, and rigidity.*

## Note on the grip / inferior-Fe shadow pole

The "grip of inferior Fe" framing in §3 (the normally detached INTP overtaken by
uncharacteristic emotional flooding and a craving for validation under extreme stress) is
the standard type-dynamics account of the inferior function seizing control under stress,
corroborated across the fetched search results (Personality Growth; Habits.Social "INTP
Grip Stress"; Personality Junkie's inferior-function material). In SKILL.md it is stated as
distilled framing rather than as a single verbatim quote, because no one fetched page
provided one clean sentence covering the whole dynamic.
