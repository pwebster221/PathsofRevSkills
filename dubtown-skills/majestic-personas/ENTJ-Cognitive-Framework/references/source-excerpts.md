## Heidi Priebe — "How Each Cognitive Function Manifests Based On Its Position In Your Stacking" (Thought Catalog, December 2015)

URL: https://thoughtcatalog.com/heidi-priebe/2015/12/how-each-cognitive-function-manifests-based-on-its-position-in-your-stacking/

Stacking chart entry: **ENTJ: Te – Ni – Se – Fi**

**Extraverted Thinking (Te) as a dominant function:**
> "As a dominant function, Te manifests as the ability to clearly envision the most
> effective outcome to any given situation or problem and the ability to set the
> corresponding plans into action."

**Introverted Intuition (Ni) as an auxiliary function:**
> "As an auxiliary function, Ni manifests as an understanding of how one ought to go
> about accomplishing his or her goals, based on a keen intuitive perception of how
> various courses of action are likely to unfold."

**Extraverted Sensing (Se) as a tertiary function:**
> "As a tertiary function, extroverted sensing manifests as the user's ability to pick
> up cues from their external environment and react to them with a sense of natural
> confidence."

**Introverted Feeling (Fi) as an inferior function:**
> "As an inferior function, Fi manifests as a general disdain for emotional expression
> and a fear of being perceived as 'weak' by others."

## Heidi Priebe — "How To Recognize Each Myers-Briggs Personality Type In Real Life" (Thought Catalog, July 2015)

URL: https://thoughtcatalog.com/heidi-priebe/2015/07/how-to-recognize-each-myers-briggs-personality-type-in-real-life/

Section: **Extroverted Thinking (Te)** — Te Dominant Types: ENTJ, ESTJ.

> "Extroverted thinking types are extremely goal oriented. They will usually ask you
> about your own goals, either subtly or overtly."

> "They enjoy talking about plans they have and the expected outcomes of those plans."

> "If you go to a Te user with a problem, they will immediately jump to solving it as
> quickly and effectively as possible."

> "Te users are bothered by inefficiency and strongly dislike people being late or
> veering off topic if there's work to be done."

> "Dominant Te types may come across as bossy or know-it-all-ish, even when they
> genuinely mean well."

> "They will always argue from a results-based, purely practical standpoint."

> "These types usually advocate for staying organized, setting goals and making
> whatever choice will glean the best outcomes."

## A.J. Drenth — "ENTJ Personality Type Profile" (Personality Junkie)

URL: https://personalityjunkie.com/entj-profile/

> "Extraverted Thinking (Te) serves as ENTJs' dominant and most preferred function. It
> undergirds their tendency to quickly express their judgments and opinions, to
> literally think (i.e., make judgments, conclusions, decisions, etc.) aloud."

> "Te strives to impose order and rationality on external world. It is quantitative in
> nature, pushing for objective standards and measurable goals."

> "ENTJs are born leaders. They are direct, assertive, and uninhibited."

## Susan Storm — "Inside the Mind of an ENTJ: A Deep Dive into Their Cognitive Functions" (Psychology Junkie)

URL: https://www.psychologyjunkie.com/inside-the-mind-of-an-entj-a-deep-dive-into-their-cognitive-functions/

**Dominant Te:**
> "Te is your wheelhouse; you live and breathe this function without really having to try."

**Auxiliary Ni:**
> "If you've ever gotten the feeling that you just know how something will play out,
> chances are you're tapped into Ni."

**Tertiary Se:**
> "When you feel like you're fully in the moment, with heightened awareness of the
> details around you, ready to jump into action, you're tapped into Se."

**Inferior Fi:**
> "ENTJs often struggle with Fi, neglecting their own feelings in favor of logical and
> pragmatic solutions."

## "How Functions Work: Inferior Fi (ESTJ/ENTJ)" (mbti-notes / Type Theory)

URL: https://mbti-notes.tumblr.com/post/140050745702/how-functions-work-inferior-fi-estjentj

> "When they become stressed and mentally fatigued to the point where Te can no longer
> maintain its dominance, unhealthy Fi urges will finally erupt from the unconscious,
> driving Te-doms to surrender to Fi in a negative or destructive manner."

> "Being accustomed to maintaining composure via suppressing private emotions, they will
> find it difficult to comprehend the eruption of those emotions into consciousness,
> becoming hypersensitive to them and feeling consumed by them."

> "They tend to feel easily hurt (by criticism), undervalued, unappreciated,
> disrespected or used by others, even perceiving themselves as a martyr or victim,
> uncharacteristically wallowing in self-pity."
