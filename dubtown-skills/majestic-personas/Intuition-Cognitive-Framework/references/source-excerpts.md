# PART I — THE OUTWARD VOICE: Extraverted Intuition (Ne)

## Source A — A.J. Drenth, "Extraverted Intuition (Ne): An Inside Look," Personality Junkie
URL: https://personalityjunkie.com/10/extraverted-intuition-ne/

> "An overarching purpose of Ne involves acquiring information from the outside world." **(re-fetched June 2026)**

> "Extraverted Intuition scans for relationships or patterns within a pool of ideas, facts, or experiences." **(re-fetched June 2026)**

> "This allows NPs to discern otherwise hidden patterns, possibilities, and potentials." **(re-fetched June 2026)**

> "While NPs' Ti or Fi pushes for closure, their Ne counters by rallying for more options and alternatives."

> "Ne also contributes a sense of wonderment toward life's mysteries and contingencies."

> "Ne confers a sense of blind anticipation, a sense of excitement regarding who or what will manifest next." **(re-fetched June 2026)**

> "Ne can contribute to a persistent sense of restlessness or dissatisfaction." **(re-fetched June 2026)**

## Source B — "ENTP Personality Type Profile," Personality Junkie
URL: https://personalityjunkie.com/entp/

> "Extraverted Intuition (Ne) is a novelty-seeking function." **(re-fetched June 2026)**

> "Ne is constantly scanning for new connections and patterns." **(re-fetched June 2026)**

> "Because it is an extraverted function, Ne is more divergent, extensive, and open-ended than Introverted Intuition (Ni)." **(re-fetched June 2026)**

> "Ne also confers open-mindedness. It helps ENTPs see truth on both sides of an issue without forming unwarranted judgments or premature conclusions."

## Source C — "A Deep-Dive Into the ENTP Personality Type," Psychology Junkie
URL: https://www.psychologyjunkie.com/the-entp/

> "Everything is connected, everything is part of a bigger picture, everything has exquisite potential and possibility."

> "ENTPs are so full of plans and so constantly tuned into possibility that in the time it takes them to brush their teeth in the morning they probably have thought up 100 ideas!"

## Source D — "ENFP Personality Type: In-Depth Profile & Analysis," Personality Junkie
URL: https://personalityjunkie.com/enfp/

> "ENFPs are enthusiastic, idealistic, restless, and open-minded, with wide-ranging interests."

> "ENFPs are novelty-seekers. They are constantly scanning for new and interesting people, ideas, and possibilities."

> "ENFPs are constantly generating new ideas, associations, and quips."

> "They rarely know in advance precisely what they are seeking, which is partly why they find it so exhilarating."

> "ENFPs are quintessential seekers and dilettantes, wanting to experience as much of life and the world as is humanly possible."

## Source E — "ENFP Primary Function: Understanding the ENFPs Extraverted Intuition (Ne)," Personality Growth
URL: https://personalitygrowth.com/enfp-primary-function-understanding-the-enfps-extraverted-intuition-ne/

> "They don't just want their lives to be stagnant and ordinary, instead they need to imagine and explore possibilities."

# PART II — THE INWARD VOICE: Introverted Intuition (Ni)

## Source F — A.J. Drenth, "Introverted Intuition (Ni): An Inside Look," Personality Junkie
URL: https://personalityjunkie.com/10/introverted-intuition-ni/

> "the lion's share of INJs' 'thinking' or cognitive processing occurs outside of their conscious awareness." **(re-fetched June 2026)**

> "Their best thinking is typically done without thinking, at least not consciously."

> "Ni is comprehensive and holistic. Its visions, answers, and insights manifest as comprehensive wholes." **(re-fetched June 2026)**

> "While not technically a judging function, Ni often functions in a convergent fashion, providing elegant answers and solutions to complex problems." **(re-fetched June 2026)**

> "the solution arrives through a single flash of insight—an 'aha!' moment."

> "They often think by way of images rather than words."

> "At a deep intuitive level, INJs experience their intuitions as true and trustworthy."

> "INJs often feel more like recipients than they do creators of their ingenious ideas." **(re-fetched June 2026)**

> "Their Ni then subconsciously processes this data in order to make sense of it, like assembling pieces of a puzzle." **(re-fetched June 2026)**

## Source G — Heidi Priebe, "How Each Cognitive Function Manifests Based On Its Position In Your Stacking," Thought Catalog (Dec 2015)
URL: https://thoughtcatalog.com/heidi-priebe/2015/12/how-each-cognitive-function-manifests-based-on-its-position-in-your-stacking/

> "Ni manifests as a keen perception for the meaningful connections that exist between the thoughts, concepts, events and occurrences in the Ni-user's environment."

> "Everything is interconnected and I must determine the meaning and implication behind those connections."

## Source H — Heidi Priebe, "How To Recognize Each Myers-Briggs Personality Type In Real Life," Thought Catalog (Jul 2015)
URL: https://thoughtcatalog.com/heidi-priebe/2015/07/how-to-recognize-each-myers-briggs-personality-type-in-real-life/

> "Everything an Ni dominant type does is deliberate."

## Source I — A.J. Drenth, "INTJ Personality Type Profile," Personality Junkie
URL: https://personalityjunkie.com/the-intj/

> "Intuitive insight often seems to emerge ex nihilo, as a welcomed but unexpected gift from the muses."

## Source J — Susan Storm, "The INTJ Cognitive Function Stack," Psychology Junkie
URL: https://www.psychologyjunkie.com/the-intj-cognitive-function-stack/

> "Introverted Intuition (Ni) gives INTJs the ability to future-prospect and witness present-day reality from a broad perspective."

> "Ni insights well up from the unconscious at its own pace, cluing the INTJ into the thematic narratives playing out in their lives."

> "Ni is often equated to seemingly out-of-nowhere 'Aha!' moments."

> "INTJs come to trust and rely on their deeper mind's ability to organize their perceptions in the background."

# PART III — THE SHADOW: intuition turned against itself, and the grip

## Ne shadow — Source K: "The ENTP Dark Side," Psychology Junkie
URL: https://www.psychologyjunkie.com/the-entp-dark-side/

> "Immature ENTPs may debate just for the sake of it and will rarely take the time to listen"

> "In their compulsion to explore new ideas, they often leave projects half-finished. Responsibilities may fall by the wayside"

## Ne shadow — Source L: Susan Storm, "The Dark Side of the ENFP Personality Type," Psychology Junkie
URL: https://www.psychologyjunkie.com/the-dark-side-of-the-enfp-personality-type/

> "they can be so seduced by a new idea or opportunity that they leave other projects unfinished"

> "ENFPs may struggle with commitment, feeling that it ties them down or limits their potential"

## Ne grip (inferior Si) — Source M: "The ENTP and Grip Stress: What it Is and How to Cope," Psychology Junkie
URL: https://www.psychologyjunkie.com/the-entp-and-grip-stress-what-it-is-and-how-to-cope/

> "But in the grip, your focus narrows drastically, and you become obsessed with small details."

## Ni shadow — Source N: Susan Storm, "10 Signs of an Unhealthy INTJ," Psychology Junkie
URL: https://www.psychologyjunkie.com/10-signs-of-an-unhealthy-intj/

> "Unhealthy INTJs get tunnel vision about their ideas and visions."

> "Unhealthy INTJs will bend the facts and the logic to fit their vision or idea."

## Ni shadow — Source O: A.J. Drenth, "The 'Other Side' of the INFJ," Personality Junkie
URL: https://personalityjunkie.com/07/infj-other-side/

> "their unswerving devotion to their own visions and intuitions causes them to be closed off to alternative opinions or arguments."

> "INFJs can be rather impervious to 'facts' – particularly facts of an Si/Te nature."

## Ni grip (inferior Se) — Source P: Susan Storm, "The Unhealthy INFJ," Psychology Junkie
URL: https://www.psychologyjunkie.com/the-unhealthy-infj/

> "Under chronic or extreme stress, INFJs may fall into the grip of their inferior function, Extraverted Sensing (Se)."

> "Instead, they're impulsive, overstimulated, and reactionary."
