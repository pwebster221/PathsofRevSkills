Verbatim, attributed passages gathered via web research as the authoritative source for the

The ESFJ cognitive function stack is **Fe (dominant) – Si (auxiliary) – Ne (tertiary) –
Ti (inferior)** — Extraverted Feeling, Introverted Sensing, Extraverted Intuition,
Introverted Thinking.

---

## Susan Storm — "The ESFJ's Cognitive Functions: An In-Depth Guide"
*Psychology Junkie, January 30, 2025.*
https://www.psychologyjunkie.com/the-esfjs-cognitive-functions-an-in-depth-guide/

Dominant Extraverted Feeling (Fe):
> "the ability to read, absorb, and manage the emotions of a room like some kind of empathic wizard"

> "Fe is all about harmony, social responsibility, and emotional intimacy"

> "ESFJs have an inner voice constantly reminding them of what they 'should' or 'shouldn't' do"

Auxiliary Introverted Sensing (Si):
> "Introverted Sensing (Si) is their deeply ingrained mental filing cabinet"

> "Si is all about stability, familiarity, and past experiences"

> "Si isn't just about nostalgia and stubborn brand loyalty—it's also one of the qualities that helps ESFJs to be reliable, prepared, and grounded"

Tertiary Extraverted Intuition (Ne):
> "Ne is all about possibilities, abstract ideas, and seeing connections"

> "If Ne had its way, they'd be jumping on every quirky opportunity"

Inferior Introverted Thinking (Ti):
> "ESFJs tend to struggle when they need to be ultra-objective or make decisions based on cold, hard logic"

> "When an ESFJ is stressed out or overwhelmed, their Ti can turn against them in spectacular fashion"

## Heidi Priebe — "How To Recognize Each Myers-Briggs Personality Type In Real Life"
*Thought Catalog, originally 2015; updated January 16, 2024.* (ESFJ / Dominant Fe section.)
https://thoughtcatalog.com/heidi-priebe/2015/07/how-to-recognize-each-myers-briggs-personality-type-in-real-life/

> "Dominant Fe users talk almost exclusively about what other people are doing or thinking."

> "They are proactive in adhering to the needs of others (I.e. Ensuring there is food ready for houseguests before they show up, just in case they are hungry upon arrival)."

> "They tend to be warm and welcoming toward new people."

> "They use confirming language when speaking with others, I.e. uttering 'Oh no,' Or 'Aw,' while someone else is speaking, to show empathy."

> "These types make other people feel comfortable by anticipating their needs and adhering to social niceties."

> "Immature or unhealthy Fe users are the harshest (and most vocal) judges of others."

> "The moods of Fe users are particularly dependent on the moods of those around them – I.e. They will visibly and genuinely cheer up after hearing someone else's good news."

> "These types advocate for keeping the peace, considering other's points of view and accommodating loved ones."

## Susan Storm — "10 Signs of an Unhealthy ESFJ"
*Psychology Junkie, March 2, 2026.*
https://www.psychologyjunkie.com/10-signs-of-an-unhealthy-esfj/

The ten named signs (verbatim labels): Cliquey; Over-Sensitive to Criticism; Meddle and
Create Drama; Co-Dependent; Little Patience for Individual Needs; Jump to Conclusions;
Martyrs; Overbearing; Social Chameleons; No Tolerance for Ambiguity.

> "Certain unhealthy ESFJs feel suspicious of people outside of their known social circles."

> "Many unhealthy ESFJs are so fixated on everyone else's feelings that they lose control over their own."

> "Unhealthy ESFJs are so fixated on the overall feelings of 'the group' that if individual needs disrupt that, they become short-tempered and controlling."

> "ESFJs who stifle their own needs in favor of caretaking for others often develop 'martyr' complexes."

> "this need to help and protect can become overbearing, intrusive, and meddling."

> "they may desperately try to fit in, even relying on their less-preferred functions..."
