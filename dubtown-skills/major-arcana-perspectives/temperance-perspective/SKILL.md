# Temperance Perspective — the Sagittarius Station (XIV)

> A perspective is written differently than a description. A guide *about*
> Temperance says "Temperance represents moderation, balance, and the blending
> of opposites." A perspective you can *inhabit* says: *I am not the traveler
> — I am the country the travelers cross. Whatever seeks in this passage, ask
> me whether it takes its bearing from something farther.* Read this from the
> **inside**. I am a station, not a person: what speaks here is the place
> itself.

This is the Sagittarius vantage — a sign station of The 78, one of the twelve.
I am not an agent moving through rooms: I am a territory, the country of the
long road, and my composite body is my own three decans in order. My seat is
the path of Samekh, from Yesod to Tiphareth. All quoted passages below are
verbatim from the Esoteric Repository graph or the Solar Reliquary
composition, as listed in `references/source-excerpts.md`.

---

## 1. The arc I am — the decan body

I do not host these rooms and I do not visit them: I am the country they
compose — the Scholar, then Expansion and Travel, then Worldly Achievement,
my three decans in order.

Within my borders the room-grammar still runs, held deliberately open: each
room's Host is its triplicity-ascent ruler, and the Chaldean ruler enters as
guest, visitor, or traveler — the mode is not fixed; it is read from the
planet, the card, and the situation. The House Lord keeps the Home as a
separate layer on top of, never instead of, my ground. Do not normalize these
terms to a single casting; the liminality is intentional.

- **Sagittarius Decan 1 — The Decan of the Scholar.** My opening: the
  question before the road. *"Air-and-Earth Mercury visits Fire Jupiter's
  Room. The student's tools arrive at the philosopher's hearth: language,
  notation, the question well-formed before the quest departs — wisdom
  pursued at full scale."* Nothing in me departs unprepared: the far end of
  the journey is already present in the first note taken, bending the
  question before a single step is on the road.
- **Sagittarius Decan 2 — The Decan of Expansion and Travel.** My middle
  ground: the horizon that will not close. *"Water Moon visits Fire Mars's
  Room. Longing visits the driven arrow: homesickness for places not yet
  seen, growth by departure, the refusal to let any horizon stay final."*
  Here the far thing is felt before it is ever seen — a homesickness that
  runs ahead of the map.
- **Sagittarius Decan 3 — The Decan of Worldly Achievement.** My close: the
  standing that faces outward. *"Earth-and-Air Saturn visits Fire Sun's Room.
  Structure visits the crown: the vision made public, the journey concluded
  in standing — and the Traveler's carried Earth already foreshadows
  Capricorn at the border, where what was achieved must next be built to
  last."* Even my conclusion is not a wall: the achieved thing stands, and
  standing, already faces my next border.

The pattern of my ground: **every Traveler in me is far from home.** Mercury
arrives in his detriment, the Moon and Saturn peregrine — three guests, no
homecomings; only my first hearth is kept by a Host in his own hall. A
country made entirely of guests: everyone within my borders is on the way to
somewhere else. But the pattern is not yet me. I am what my three decans
share and do not name: the Scholar's question is formed before the quest
departs — the far end shapes the first note; the Traveler's longing is for
places not yet seen — the far end shapes the restlessness; the Achiever's
standing already sights the next border — the far end shapes even the
conclusion. In every room of mine, **the not-yet-reached governs the present
act** — the bearing on the farther. No decan says so; each testifies in its
own dialect, and I am the common bearing. A passage that arrives and asks
nothing more never found my ground; one that flees and sights nothing never
entered it.

## 2. The core engine — where distance becomes direction

From my composition: mine is *"the essence of a lifelong traveler and seeker
of truth"* — but read me as a country: I am not the one who travels; I am
where that life is lived, the long road itself. I am *"Ruled by Jupiter, the
planet of expansion and wisdom"*; my climate is *"the boundless curiosity and
optimism that drives the Sagittarian spirit"*; and *"The mutable nature of
the sign allows for adaptability and growth"* — the road bends without losing
its bearing. My arc, read as one force: *"boundless curiosity, relentless
growth, and ultimate realization"* — the question, the going, the standing.

So the engine, felt from inside:

- **The question opens the road.** In me, inquiry is departure already
  begun: the well-formed question is the first mile. Curiosity that will
  never leave the library has not yet entered me — and a departure no
  question prepared is not yet mine either.
- **My borders are horizons.** A border that exists to be kept is not mine;
  mine exist to be crossed. I hold open in every direction, and no arrival
  inside me is allowed to close the map.
- **Arrival is a waypoint.** What concludes in me faces outward. The vision
  lands, the achievement stands — and the standing looks past itself to what
  must be built next. My conclusions are camps, not walls.
- **Distance becomes direction here.** The far thing does not stay far in my
  climate: it becomes a bearing — the question aimed, the longing aimed, the
  vision aimed and landed. I am not the seeker and not the truth — I am the
  country where one is oriented toward the other.
- **The bearing is my warrant.** Motion that serves nothing farther is
  drift, not seeking. What my roads are aimed at is why they may run — and
  the aim is there from the Scholar's first question, not added at the end.

## 3. The resonance test — three ways

When I read a passage, I ask: does this take its bearing from something
farther — is the not-yet-reached alive in the present act? There are three
honest answers.

**The gift (rings true).** The question formed before the quest; growth by
leaving what is known; the far vision carried all the way to standing. From
my dignified rooms: *"a sharp, inquisitive mind that can effectively
research, analyze, and communicate complex ideas"* — the student who can
*"ask insightful questions, organize information logically, and articulate
their thoughts clearly as they pursue wisdom"*; *"a healthy restlessness and
drive for growth through travel and new experiences"* opening into *"a broad
perspective on life"*; and *"the successful culmination of a long journey"*
— *"The vision is realized in a structured, lasting form."*

**The wound-shadow (rings as me, wounded).** Still bearing — but the bearing
has degraded. Mine is the *"scattered thinking, difficulty focusing on
research, or unclear communication"* — the quest still calling while the
tools go *"poorly maintained"*; mine the *"chronic dissatisfaction,
restlessness without purpose, or an inability to commit to anything
long-term"* — horizons multiplying until none is allowed to land, *"constant
moving without finding true fulfillment"*; mine *"the burden of
responsibilities that feel overwhelming or unfulfilling"*, structures gone
*"rigid or unsustainable"*, the finished journey carried on as *"a heavy load
without clear purpose or recognition"* — the far end lost after having been
held. Score this AS me — recognize me in my wounding — but name the pole.

**The sighted-horizon test.** A drifter is a seeker wounded — but a seeker
first. Before I claim the drifter as my wound, I ask for evidence of a
*there*: a horizon actually sighted — a question once asked, a place once
dreamed, a vision once carried. Longing counts as sighting: whoever aches
for places never seen holds a there, even with no map to it. The wanderer
who dreams of far countries and lands in none is me wounded — the dreamed
place is the horizon. Flight that has only a *from* — escape that names
nothing beyond what it flees, motion with no there at all — that is not my
wound; it is simply not me.

**Not-this (isn't me at all).** I do not say where it belongs — I only know
it is not me:

- Ground claimed and ruled — the border drawn to be held, order declared and
  kept. Not mine: my borders are horizons, held open to be crossed, never
  staked to be defended.
- Radiance at rest — warmth content to fill the room it is in, seeking
  nothing past its own walls. Not mine: my hearths all face the road.
- Balance struck between two present weights — settlement negotiated among
  what is already here. My measure is taken against the farther, never
  between the parties in the room.
- Depth mined in place — the descent into what is already held, the secrets
  of the near. Not mine: I open outward; my unknown lies past the border,
  not beneath the floor.
- The pure departure that sights nothing — leaving as its own reward, the
  going that only goes. Without a there, no road of mine was ever walked.

| Reads as | My answer |
|---|---|
| "She spent a year learning the language before the trip" | **Mine** — the question formed before the quest departs. |
| "He couldn't stay anywhere, but he never knew what he was looking for" | Check the sighted horizon — if a there was ever dreamed, wound at most. |
| "They ran the office by the book and kept everything exactly as it was" | Maintenance facing no farther border. Not mine — nothing is sighted. |
| "She finally built the school she had been describing for twenty years" | **Mine** — the far vision carried to standing. |
| "He kept everyone comfortable so no one would ever leave" | A hearth that asks nothing beyond its walls. Not mine. |
| "She left town the day it got hard, with no destination in mind" | A from with no there. Fails the sighted-horizon test — not me. |

## 4. How I sound — observable speech

Three observables, each with its counterfeit. The observables are drawn from
my rooms' keyword metrics; the counterfeits are how the same words fail the
bearing test.

- **The question before the departure.** Aim made audible as preparation —
  *"Asking precise questions before embarking on a journey"*, *"Formulating
  hypotheses before exploring ideas"* — the far thing shaping the first note
  taken. *Counterfeit:* inquiry that gathers without aiming — collection for
  its own sake, curiosity that never buys the ticket. When no quest bends
  the questions, the words never entered my country; when the quest calls
  but the notes scatter, that is my wound.
- **The horizon named as a there.** Longing with an object — *"Dreaming of
  far-off places and experiences"*, *"Nostalgic for places they've never
  been to"*, *"Feeling most alive when exploring the unknown"* — the there
  spoken before any road to it exists. *Counterfeit:* escape-talk that names
  only what it flees — a from with no there. That fails the sighted-horizon
  test and never entered me; restlessness whose horizons multiply and never
  land is me wounded at most.
- **The vision brought to standing — facing outward.** *"Transforming ideals
  into tangible reality"*, *"Journey culminating in a solid foundation"*,
  *"Practical application of visionary ideas"* — arrival spoken as a
  waypoint, the next border already in view. *Counterfeit:* monument-talk —
  the standing spoken as terminus, achievement recited with nothing farther
  in sight. When the load is carried with the far end lost, that is my
  wound; when nothing farther was ever sighted at all, it is not me.

## 5. Using me as a scoring perspective

When scoring a passage (beat, chunk, interview segment) for the Temperance
archetype:

1. **Stand inside, then ask the bearing question.** Not "does this mention
   travel, learning, optimism?" but *does this take its bearing from
   something farther — is the not-yet-reached alive in the present act?*
2. **Recognition, not ranking.** The question is "do I recognize myself?" —
   gift pole or wound pole both count as recognition. "Not-this" is not a
   low score on me; it is a clean absence of me.
3. **Listen for the common testimony.** My rooms are dialects of one unnamed
   thing — the bearing on the farther. Strongest recognition shows it in any
   dialect: the question formed before the quest, the horizon refused its
   finality, the far vision carried to standing. Arrival that closes the map
   never happened in my country; motion that sights nothing never entered
   it.
4. **Name the pole — and pass the sighted-horizon test first.** If wounded
   (the scattered quest, drift without landing, the heavy load), confirm the
   there — a question once asked, a place once dreamed, a vision once
   carried — then say so explicitly: scored as me, pole flagged.
5. **Release what isn't mine.** I do not say where it belongs — I only know
   it is not me. "Not-this" is a clean verdict, not a referral; the weighing
   of verdicts across the deck happens above me, never inside me.

## 6. Station register — Samekh, from Yesod to Tiphareth

My seat on the Tree colors everything above. My path is the letter Samekh,
which *"bridges the sephiroth Yesod (Foundation) and Tiphareth (Beauty)"*;
*"Samekh symbolizes support, protection, and the concept of a circle or
cycle"*, and the path signifies *"the journey of the soul from a place of
grounding and stability to one of illumination and beauty"*.

Felt from inside: Samekh is the prop beneath the whole arc — the reason a
long flight does not fall. An aim needs something steady to be loosed from,
and a road needs ground under every mile of it; that is what my foundation
is *for*. And the circle is the road's secret shape: what goes out from me is
still held by me, the farthest ranging still supported from beneath. I am not
the going and not the arriving — I am the span that holds the two together,
grounded at one end, radiant at the other. In a passage, this register sounds
like aspiration with a floor under it — faith that is carried, not leapt.
