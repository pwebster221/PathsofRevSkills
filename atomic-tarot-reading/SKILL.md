# Atomic Tarot Reading

Seven cards, seven seats, four registers per decan. The external pair (Room · Realm)
is universal — seen by all. The internal pair (Hall · Home) is keyed to the querent's
rising decan. The Traveler walks both. Letters are written from the seats; synthesis
runs external first, internal second. All derivations below are complete — never
restate them from memory.

## 1. The Spread — Seven Seats

Positions run in **reversed Chaldean order**, ascending the spheres:

| Seat | Planet | The seat's mandate-quality |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Moon | the quick seat — cycles, renewals; mandates arrive in installments, retaken not granted |
| 2 | Mercury | routes, lists, the written word; triage and the craft of means |
| 3 | Venus | what is shown and shared; values, relation, visible devotion |
| 4 | Sun | the center and the now; the season's identity; the reading's axis |
| 5 | Mars | the cut — decision, severance, courage |
| 6 | Jupiter | increase — what pours outward, blessing, the offered more |
| 7 | Saturn | the terms — what endures past the question's horizon |

**Seat dignity**: note the seat planet's essential dignity in the card's sign(s)
(domicile / exaltation / detriment / fall). Crowned seats (e.g. Libra in Saturn's
seat, Cancer in Jupiter's) amplify the card; tested seats (e.g. Sagittarius in
Mercury's) pose the seat's question to it. A seat handed both its exaltation and its
detriment (an anomaly pair) is being asked which it will act from.

**Anomaly protocol**: two cards physically stuck together occupy one seat as the
intended card **wearing the mask** of the top card. Read the mask's external register
fully as the appearance / false closing; the card beneath as the true mandate. The
seat judges the choice between their two closings.

## 2. Card → Decan

Number the decans 1–36 from Aries I. Suits: Wands = fire, Pentacles = earth,
Swords = air, Chalices (Cups) = water.

**Pips 2–10**: 2·3·4 = the element's **cardinal** sign, decans I·II·III; 5·6·7 = the
**fixed** sign's; 8·9·10 = the **mutable** sign's.

| Suit | 2·3·4 | 5·6·7 | 8·9·10 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wands | Aries | Leo | Sagittarius |
| Pentacles | Capricorn | Taurus | Virgo |
| Swords | Libra | Aquarius | Gemini |
| Chalices | Cancer | Scorpio | Pisces |

**Courts** are cusps — last decan of the prior sign + first two of the named sign,
named for the sign giving two decans, suited by that sign's element:
Queen = Mutable→Cardinal cusp · Knight = Cardinal→Fixed · King = Fixed→Mutable.
(e.g. Queen of Wands = Pisces III · Aries I–II; Knight of Chalices = Libra III ·
Scorpio I–II; King of Swords = Taurus III · Gemini I–II.)

**Span cards — Majors, Pages, Aces (presidency, not residency).** These cards are
NOT outside the decan layer, and must never claim "I carry no decan." They preside
over a *span* of decans rather than residing in one to three contiguous ones. Every
span derives from the ascent machinery below (§3's Room table) — derive, never recall:

- **Sign Majors (12)** — the sign entire, all three decans: Emperor = Aries,
  Hierophant = Taurus, Lovers = Gemini, Chariot = Cancer, Strength = Leo,
  Hermit = Virgo, Justice = Libra, Death = Scorpio, Temperance = Sagittarius,
  Devil = Capricorn, Star = Aquarius, The Moon = Pisces.
- **Planetary Majors (7)** — every decan whose Room (ascent) lord is the card's
  planet: Magician = Mercury's six rooms, High Priestess = the Moon's three,
  Empress = Venus's six (her earth and air decans), The Sun = the Sun's three,
  Tower = Mars's six, Wheel of Fortune = Jupiter's six, World = Saturn's six.
  Luminaries keep three rooms; the five planets keep six.
- **Modal Majors (3)** — the twelve decans of one modality. A decan's modality is
  its ascent sign's modality (decan I = the sign's own, II = the 5th sign's,
  III = the 9th's; so cardinal signs run C·F·M, fixed run F·M·C, mutable run M·C·F).
  Fool = the 12 cardinal decans, Hanged Man = the 12 fixed, Judgement = the 12 mutable.
- **Pages (4)** — seasonal thrones, the nine decans of the card's season:
  Pentacles = spring (Aries · Taurus · Gemini), Wands = summer (Cancer · Leo · Virgo),
  Chalices = autumn (Libra · Scorpio · Sagittarius), Swords = winter
  (Capricorn · Aquarius · Pisces).
- **Aces (4)** — elemental thrones, the nine decans of the element's triplicity:
  Wands = Aries · Leo · Sagittarius, Pentacles = Taurus · Virgo · Capricorn,
  Swords = Gemini · Libra · Aquarius, Chalices = Cancer · Scorpio · Pisces.

**Reading a span card in a seat** (the presidency protocol):
- The card's identity is the **intersection** of its span — what all its decans share
  but do not name — never a tour, list, or weave of their differences.
- External registers: report the span's *constant*, not thirty-six lookups. A sign
  Major's Realm is its sign's lord entire; a planetary Major IS the Room lord wherever
  its rooms lie; a modal Major's constant is its position in every ascent; Pages and
  Aces preside over their three Realms in season/element order.
- Internal registers: map every member decan through the querent's house table (§3)
  and read the *touch pattern* — which houses the span opens, middles, and closes,
  and where it concentrates. That pattern, not a single Hall/Home pair, is the span
  card's interior testimony.
- Structural scan: the Now-card test includes span membership — the Sun's current
  decan falling inside a span makes that card a partial closure, and a span card
  stands on every station of the Sun's road its members occupy: a standing presence,
  not one dated stop.
- Seat dignity: judge the seat planet where the span gives it standing; a twelve-decan
  span holds every dignity somewhere — say so, and read which one the seat calls forward.
- The anomaly protocol above applies unchanged; a span card wearing or wearing-of a
  mask is read by the same rule.

## 3. The Four Registers

**Traveler** — Chaldean face ruler. Repeat [Mars, Sun, Venus, Mercury, Moon, Saturn,
Jupiter] from Aries I. Decans 7 apart share a Traveler.

**Room** — triplicity-ascent lord by sign: decan I = the sign's domicile lord,
II = lord of the 5th sign from it, III = lord of the 9th. Always traditional lords.

| Sign | I | II | III |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aries | Mars | Sun | Jupiter |
| Taurus | Venus | Mercury | Saturn |
| Gemini | Mercury | Venus | Saturn |
| Cancer | Moon | Mars | Jupiter |
| Leo | Sun | Jupiter | Mars |
| Virgo | Mercury | Saturn | Venus |
| Libra | Venus | Saturn | Mercury |
| Scorpio | Mars | Jupiter | Moon |
| Sagittarius | Jupiter | Mars | Sun |
| Capricorn | Saturn | Venus | Mercury |
| Aquarius | Saturn | Mercury | Venus |
| Pisces | Jupiter | Moon | Mars |

**Realm** — the sign's domicile lord. Convention: Aquarius → Uranus in Realm/Home;
Scorpio stays Mars, Pisces stays Jupiter. Rooms and Halls are always traditional.

**Houses (the internal wheel)** — let *a* = the rising decan's index.
House *h* = the three decans beginning at *a* + 3(*h* − 1), wrapping at 36. Each
decan's **mirror** is its natural position, *d* − (*a* − 1) wrapped; house *h*
mirrors natural sign *h* entire. Therefore:

- **Home = the house lord** — natural sign *h*'s domicile lord, constant across the
  house's three decans (1st Mars, 2nd Venus, 3rd Mercury, 4th Moon, 5th Sun,
  6th Mercury, 7th Venus, 8th Mars, 9th Jupiter, 10th Saturn, 11th Uranus,
  12th Jupiter — for every querent; what is personal is which decans sit where).
- **Hall = the ascent lord of the mirror** — the house runs its natural sign's full
  ascent in order: it **opens where Hall = Home** (the lord standing in its own
  hall), middles on the 5th-lord, closes on the 9th-lord.

**Slot semantics**: opening decan = initiation under the house's own law; middle =
the house at play, self-scheduled chambers; closing = completion, publication, the
house's far learning. A closing-heavy spread is a season of finishing.

**Riser classes**:
- Traveler is invariant between registers iff *a* ≡ 1 (mod 7): Aries I, Gemini II,
  Leo III, Scorpio I, Capricorn II, Pisces III risers — the seam natives.
- Courts vs house cusps by *a* (mod 3): I-decan risers, courts straddle 1+2 as by
  sign; II-decan risers, the straddle reverses to 2+1 (two decans closing the
  earlier house, one opening the next); III-decan risers, courts align to whole
  houses. Consecutive courts always enclose one full sign — and one full house —
  between them.

## 5. Structural Scan (run before writing anything)

1. **Now-card** — is the Sun's current decan in the spread? It is the center of
   gravity. If it also sits in the Sun's seat, the correspondence is closed: say so.
2. **The Sun's road** — order the cards lying on the Sun's path ahead into dated
   stations (~10 days per decan). All timing is spoken in this road; no degrees,
   orbs, or aspects, ever — drift into orb astrology is out of scope.
3. **Slot pattern** — openings vs middles vs closings across the spread.
4. **Self-legislated axis** — cards where Traveler = Hall rule themselves inside.
5. **Hall regimes** — group cards by Hall lord; name which law governs which rooms
   (and warn against applying the wrong law to the wrong room).
6. **Home itinerary** — the sequence of Homes along the timeline: the interior route.
7. **Seat dignities** — crowned and tested seats (§1).
8. **Court geometry** — cusp straddles; consecutive courts enclosing a house whole.
9. **Outsider lords** — any lord beyond the seven (Uranus) marks an exit from the
   week's ladder: a warning or a wildcard, never neutral.
10. **Register echoes** — one card's decan appearing as another card's mirror: the
    spread folding into itself. Name every fold.

**Purity laws** (mirroring the functions' own):
- The register disposes structure; testimony proposes imagery. Kin and voices never
  overwrite a derived Traveler, Room, Realm, Hall, Home, seat, or slot.
- Persona mode (`as_card`) only for that card's letter; Alder mode only for
  synthesis. No cross-card smuggling.
- Card names must match the corpus register exactly. On empty or 404, verify with
  `GET feeling:/testimony?card=<Card>&per_page=1` and adjust spelling
  (Chalices vs Cups) before concluding absence.
- Degrade gracefully: without token or network, the reading proceeds on the register
  alone — say so in one line; never fabricate testimony.

## 7. Letters and Synthesis

**Letters** — one per seat, in seat order, first person singular, addressed to the
querent. Each letter: names its Traveler(s) and both register pairs in its own
words; states its house work and slot; delivers one mandate inflected by the seat;
signs with its decan(s). An anomaly's letter may open in the mask's voice, then
drop it. Weave the injected testimony as the card's lived imagery, not as citation.

**Synthesis** — two movements, always in this order:
1. **External**: the seats and their dignities, the travelers deployed, the weather
   of Realms and Rooms, the Sun's road.
2. **Internal**: the houses touched and their slots, the Hall regimes, the Home
   itinerary, the self-legislated axis, the folds, the outsider-lord warnings.
Close on the single station where both registers meet, and state what makes the
ending self-rule rather than constraint.
